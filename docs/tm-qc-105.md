# TM-QC-105 — Bacterial Endotoxins Test, FL-101 Drug Substance

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Control

## 1. Purpose

This method determines the bacterial endotoxin content of FL-101 drug
substance by the kinetic chromogenic Limulus amebocyte lysate (LAL)
test, following USP <85>, Bacterial Endotoxins Test.

## 2. Principle

Endotoxin activates the LAL clotting cascade. In the kinetic
chromogenic format the activated enzyme cleaves a chromogenic
substrate, and the rate of color development, measured
photometrically, is proportional to endotoxin concentration against a
standard curve prepared from reference standard endotoxin.

## 3. Parameters

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Technique | Kinetic chromogenic LAL |
| Compendial basis | USP <85>, Bacterial Endotoxins Test |
| Standard | Reference standard endotoxin, standard curve prepared with each run |
| Diluent | LAL reagent water |
| Controls | Negative control and positive product control (sample spiked with endotoxin) with each run |
| Reporting | EU/mg, to two significant figures, per SOP-QA-002 |

## 4. System suitability

1. The absolute value of the standard curve correlation coefficient
   is ≥ 0.980.
2. The positive product control recovery is 50–200% of the spiked
   amount.
3. The negative control reads below the lowest standard of the curve.

A run that does not meet every criterion in this section yields no
reportable result.

## 5. Procedure (summary)

5.1 Equilibrate the sample to room temperature.

5.2 Prepare the endotoxin standard series in LAL reagent water.

5.3 Dilute the sample in LAL reagent water.

5.4 Prepare the positive product control by spiking a sample aliquot
with endotoxin.

5.5 Prepare the negative control from LAL reagent water.

5.6 Add LAL reagent to each preparation.

5.7 Read the reactions kinetically in the photometric reader.

5.8 Calculate the endotoxin concentration of each preparation from
the standard curve.

5.9 Record all readings and the curve on the raw data record.

## 6. Result and reporting

The result in EU/mg = the measured endotoxin concentration (EU/mL)
multiplied by the dilution factor, divided by the protein
concentration of the lot in mg/mL as determined under TM-QC-101.
Report to two significant figures per SOP-QA-002. Record per
SOP-QA-001 and SOP-QC-006.

This procedure is a compendial procedure verified under SOP-QC-007,
Analytical Method Transfer and Compendial Verification, before use.

Acceptance of the reported result is per SPEC-101, FL-101 Drug
Substance Specification. This method states no acceptance criterion
for the product.
