# SOP-QA-023 — Warehouse and Distribution Control

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Manufacturing

## 1. Purpose

Establish how materials and finished products are received, stored, picked, and distributed under controlled conditions, so that identity, status, and storage conditions are maintained through the supply chain.

## 2. Scope

Applies to warehousing and distribution operations for materials and finished products at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Warehouse and distribution, Quality Assurance.

Does not cover transport qualification of specific shipping lanes, which is assessed per lane, or direct-to-patient distribution models.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Warehouse Operator**
  - Receive, store, pick, and dispatch materials and finished products in accordance with this procedure.
  - Record each activity in FL-EDMS when performed.
  - Keep items separated by status.
  - Report discrepancies or storage-condition concerns to the Warehouse Supervisor the same business day.

- **Warehouse Supervisor**
  - Oversee warehouse activities.
  - Maintain the authorized-access list for storage areas.
  - Review receipt and dispatch records.
  - Escalate product or quality concerns to Quality Assurance.

- **Quality Assurance**
  - Provide quality oversight of warehouse and distribution controls.
  - Review quality concerns.
  - Make decisions on product status and disposition together with Quality Control.
  - Release materials for use.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Receive materials and finished products

1. Protect materials and finished products from weather at receiving bays.
2. For incoming materials, use approved sources and the agreed supply chain. Materials are received only from suppliers on the Approved Supplier List under SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality.
3. The receiver verifies the supplier against the list at receipt.
4. Clean incoming material containers before storage when necessary. Containers are cleaned before storage when visibly soiled or when the receipt check identifies a contamination risk.
5. The Warehouse Supervisor decides whether cleaning is required.
6. Check each receipt for identity, shipment condition, quantity, and required storage conditions.
7. Record the checks in the material receipt record in FL-EDMS.
8. Report damage or any other discrepancy to Quality Assurance the same day and handle it under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
9. Warehouse assigns the initial status Quarantine on receipt.
10. Warehouse records the initial status Quarantine in FL-EDMS before storage.

### 4.2 Assign and control status

1. Store materials and products in an orderly way that keeps different categories and statuses distinguishable.
2. Provide enough storage capacity and arrange it to reduce the risk of confusion.
3. When physical separation is used to maintain quarantine, clearly mark the quarantine area.
4. When physical separation is used to maintain quarantine, restrict access to authorised personnel. A non-physical quarantine system must provide equivalent security.
5. Provide segregated storage for rejected, recalled, and returned materials or products.
6. Returned product is held in the segregated area and is dispositioned by Quality Assurance only.
7. Status categories are Quarantine, Hold, Released, Rejected, Returned, and Recalled.
8. Quality Assurance assigns and changes material status on release for use.
9. Quality Assurance assigns and changes batch status under SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release.
10. Every status decision is recorded in FL-EDMS.
11. Status is shown by physical status labeling.
12. Status is checked in FL-EDMS before picking and before dispatch.

### 4.3 Store under controlled conditions

1. Keep storage areas clean and dry, and maintain them within acceptable temperature limits.
2. Provide, check, and monitor any required special storage conditions, including temperature or humidity.
3. Storage conditions and limits for each material and product are those of its specification — for FL-101 drug substance, frozen at −20 °C ± 5 °C. Conditions are monitored continuously by the building monitoring system with alarms.
4. When a storage-condition excursion is identified, record the excursion and the affected items.
5. When a storage-condition excursion is identified, place the affected items on Hold.
6. When a storage-condition excursion is identified, notify the Warehouse Supervisor and Quality Assurance the same day.
7. Assess the excursion under SOP-QA-010.
8. Arrange materials and products to support orderly storage and reduce the risk of mix-ups or cross-contamination.
9. Store highly active materials and products in safe and secure areas.
10. Store printed packaging materials securely, in the access-controlled labeling storage area under SOP-QA-022, Labeling and Packaging Control.
11. Prevent unauthorised entry to storage areas. Access is restricted to the authorized personnel list maintained by the Warehouse Supervisor, and storage areas are not used as passageways.
12. Keep storage-area lighting, temperature, humidity, and ventilation appropriate so they do not adversely affect products or equipment.
13. Maintain storage premises so repair and maintenance work does not create a hazard to product quality. Area cleaning follows the facility cleaning schedule maintained by Warehouse.
14. Personnel report area or equipment defects to the Warehouse Supervisor.
15. Equipment defects are raised as work orders in FL-EDMS under SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance.

### 4.4 Pick materials and finished products

1. Pick oldest approved stock first.
2. Before completing a pick, verify the item's identity, Released status, and quantity against the pick request in FL-EDMS, together with any applicable storage or handling conditions.
3. Product dispatch picks receive a second-person verification.
4. Document each pick in FL-EDMS.
5. A shortage, mismatch, or other discrepancy stops the pick.
6. Notify the Warehouse Supervisor of a shortage, mismatch, or other discrepancy.
7. Handle any product impact under SOP-QA-010.

### 4.5 Prepare and dispatch products

1. Protect materials and finished products from weather at dispatch bays.
2. Before dispatch, the Warehouse Operator checks product identity, status, quantity, destination, and required storage conditions. Only Released product is distributed.
3. The Warehouse Supervisor confirms the checks are complete.
4. Record the results and the distribution record — consignee, lot, quantity, and date — in FL-EDMS; distribution records support recall traceability under SOP-QA-014, Product Recall.
5. A dispatch discrepancy, or evidence that required storage conditions were not maintained, stops the dispatch: hold the affected product.
6. Escalate a dispatch discrepancy, or evidence that required storage conditions were not maintained, to the Warehouse Supervisor and Quality Assurance the same day for disposition under SOP-QA-010. Transport qualification of specific shipping lanes is outside this procedure's scope.

## 5. Records

Warehouse and distribution records, including material receipt records, status records, storage monitoring and excursion records, pick and dispatch records, and distribution records, retained in FL-EDMS under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
