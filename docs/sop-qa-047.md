# SOP-QA-047 — Electronic System Downtime and Paper Backup

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

This instruction describes how to issue downtime forms, record on paper when FL-EDMS or another GMP system is unavailable, and transcribe and reconcile those records after the system is restored.
This instruction sits under SOP-QA-018, Computer System Validation and Data Integrity, and SOP-QA-003, Document Control.

## 2. Issue downtime forms

1. Confirm FL-EDMS is unavailable.
2. Confirm whether another GMP system is unavailable.
3. Identify the records that must still be made during the downtime.
4. Request downtime forms from Document Control for those records.
5. Receive the downtime forms issued by Document Control.
6. Check each downtime form is numbered.
7. Confirm each downtime form number follows the format DT-<YYYY>-<NNN>.
8. Use a downtime form for each record that must still be made.
9. Keep the downtime forms for use during the downtime.
10. Record the downtime form number for each record to be made.
11. Check the downtime form number against the format DT-<YYYY>-<NNN> before use.
12. Handle the issue of downtime forms under SOP-QA-003, Document Control.

## 3. Record on paper during downtime

1. Take a numbered downtime form for the record to be made.
2. Enter the record on the downtime form.
3. Make each paper entry following SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
4. Correct a paper entry with a single line-through correction.
5. Apply the N/A convention where an entry does not apply.
6. Complete each required entry on the downtime form.
7. Keep each completed downtime form as a paper original.
8. Keep the paper originals together for transcription after restoration.
9. Check each paper entry follows SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
10. Check each correction on paper is a single line-through correction.
11. Check the N/A convention has been applied where an entry does not apply.
12. Handle a downtime that causes a data loss under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
13. Handle a downtime that causes a missed GMP record under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

## 4. Transcribe and reconcile after restoration

1. Confirm the system has been restored.
2. Take the paper originals made during the downtime.
3. Transcribe each entry made on paper into the system.
4. Transcribe each downtime form entry in turn.
5. Complete the transcription within 2 business days of restoration.
6. Reconcile each transcribed entry against the paper original.
7. Reconcile each downtime form in turn against its paper original.
8. Complete the reconciliation within 2 business days of restoration.
9. Have Quality Assurance verify the reconciliation.
10. Retain the paper originals after transcription.
11. Retain the paper originals after reconciliation.
12. File the retained paper originals in the QA Records Archive (Room 214).
13. File each downtime form original in the QA Records Archive (Room 214).
14. Handle a downtime that caused a data loss under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
15. Handle a downtime that caused a missed GMP record under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

## 5. Control of this instruction

1. Quality Assurance and the Quality Assurance Supervisor approve this instruction and each revision of it.
2. Make each revision through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
