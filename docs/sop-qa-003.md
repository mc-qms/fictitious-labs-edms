# SOP-QA-003 — Document Control

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 2.0 · Effective 2026-11-22 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

Establish how quality-system documents are drafted, reviewed, approved, issued, revised, and retired, so that only current, approved documents are in use.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the creation, approval, distribution, revision, and retirement of controlled documents at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Assurance, Quality Control laboratory, Manufacturing.

Does not cover records retention schedules for specific record types, which are set in the procedures that create those records.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Document Author**
  - Prepare document drafts and proposed revisions, and submit them for review through the document lifecycle.

- **Document Owner**
  - Maintain the document's content and coordinate its review and revision.

- **Approvers**
  - Review and approve or reject documents within their assigned authority. For laboratory control documents covered by 21 CFR § 211.160(a), Quality Assurance reviews and approves the documents and changes. Quality Assurance is responsible for approving or rejecting procedures and specifications that affect a drug product's identity, strength, quality, or purity (21 CFR § 211.22(c)).

- **Quality Assurance**
  - Administer the document system and coordinate document routing, issuance, and withdrawal. Document Control (within Quality Assurance) administers FL-EDMS and assigns every document number. Quality Assurance's system-administration role does not replace Quality Assurance's responsibilities and authority under 21 CFR §§ 211.22(c) and 211.160(a).

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Draft documents

To initiate a controlled document, the author requests a document number from Document Control, which assigns the number under the company's numbering scheme: policies, POL-<number>; procedures, SOP-<function>-<number>; work instructions, WI-<function>-<number>; test methods, TM-<function>-<number>; forms, FRM-<function>-<number>; specifications, SPEC-<function>-<number>; plans, PLN-<number>. Function codes are QA (Quality Assurance), QC (Quality Control), RA (Regulatory Affairs), and MF (Manufacturing); Document Control assigns a code to any further function when its first document is initiated. The Validation Master Plan was numbered VMP-001 before this scheme was established and retains that number. The author selects the document type and the standard format for that type, prepares the draft in FL-EDMS, and identifies the document owner and the reviewers before submitting the draft for review. Drafts are marked DRAFT in FL-EDMS and are never effective.

For laboratory control specifications, standards, sampling plans, test procedures, and other laboratory control mechanisms, the organizational unit responsible for the activity the document governs drafts the document; laboratory control documents are drafted by the Quality Control laboratory. Quality Assurance reviews and approves these documents and any changes to them. Document required laboratory-control activities when they are performed (21 CFR § 211.160(a)). Controlled documents are created, routed, and stored in FL-EDMS, the company's controlled document repository.

### 4.2 Review documents

The document owner selects reviewers from each function the document affects, together with Quality Assurance. Reviewers record their comments in FL-EDMS; the author and owner resolve each comment with the reviewer who raised it, and unresolved comments are escalated to the QA Manager. Review is complete when each reviewer records approval of the reviewed draft in FL-EDMS.

### 4.3 Approve documents

Approval follows review: the document owner approves first, then Quality Assurance. Quality-system SOPs are also approved by the QA Manager. No document becomes effective without Quality Assurance approval. Approvals are recorded by electronic signature in FL-EDMS, showing the signer's name, the date, and the meaning of the signature. The effective date is the approval date, or, where training on the document is required before use, the date that training is complete.

Quality Assurance has responsibility and authority to approve or reject procedures and specifications that affect a drug product's identity, strength, quality, or purity (21 CFR § 211.22(c)).

### 4.4 Issue and distribute documents

Document Control issues each approved document in FL-EDMS with the status Effective. The current approved version of a document is the version FL-EDMS shows as Effective; any other version — superseded, draft, or printed — must not be used. Printed copies are uncontrolled after the day they are printed. When a new version becomes effective, Document Control removes the superseded version from points of use.

### 4.5 Revise documents

A revision is requested, evaluated, and approved before implementation, through change control (SOP-QA-004, Change Control); the change request number is recorded in the document's revision history. The document owner owns the revision decision and coordinates the revised draft through the review and approval steps of Sections 4.2 and 4.3. Document Control issues the revised version in FL-EDMS, communicates the revision to the functions the document affects, and records the revision history, including the reason for the change, in FL-EDMS.

### 4.6 Retire documents

A document is retired when its owner and Quality Assurance agree it is no longer needed. Document Control withdraws the document from use in FL-EDMS, marks it Superseded/retired, and records the retirement and its reason in the document's history. One archived copy of each retired or superseded version is retained in FL-EDMS for the retention period in SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.

### 4.7 Periodic review

The document owner reviews every controlled document at least every 2 years and records the outcome — still current, revise, or retire — in FL-EDMS.

## 5. Records

Controlled documents and their revision histories, approval records, and periodic review records, retained in FL-EDMS under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
