# SOP-QA-031 — Biosafety, Biohazard Waste, and Spill Response

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes BSL-1 containment practices for work with the FL-101 production cell line and its culture and harvest materials.
It also describes how biohazard waste is decontaminated and disposed of, how spills are responded to, and how personnel exposure is reported.

## 2. Scope

Applies to biosafety, biohazard waste handling, and spill response for FL-101 at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Assurance, Quality Control laboratory, Manufacturing.

Does not cover chemical safety, general occupational safety programs, or facility cleaning and sanitization, which are outside this procedure. Does not cover cell bank storage and handling themselves, which are governed by SOP-QC-004, Cell Bank Management.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Quality Assurance**

- Own this procedure.
- Provide oversight of the biosafety, biohazard waste, and spill response program.
- Review escalated spills for product or personnel impact.
- Review personnel exposure reports.

**QA Manager**

- Approve escalated spill dispositions.
- Review deviations raised under this procedure.
- Confirm required follow-up actions are assigned.

**Laboratory Supervisor**

- Maintain spill kits in the Quality Control laboratory.
- Confirm analysts assigned to spill cleanup are trained.
- Review biohazard waste records generated in the laboratory.

**Microbiology Supervisor**

- Schedule weekly biological-indicator testing of the autoclave kill cycle.
- Review biological-indicator test results.
- Escalate a failed biological-indicator result to Quality Assurance.

**Manufacturing Supervisor**

- Maintain spill kits in the production suites.
- Confirm personnel assigned to spill cleanup are trained.
- Escalate spills outside containment to Quality Assurance.

**Analyst**

- Follow BSL-1 containment practices during assigned work.
- Place biohazard waste in the designated containers.
- Report a spill to the area supervisor.
- Report personnel exposure to the supervisor the same business day.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Apply BSL-1 containment practices

1. Confirm training is current under SOP-QA-005, Training and Qualification.
2. Wear the required personal protective equipment for the area.
3. Handle the CHO-derived production cell line at BSL-1 containment practices.
4. Handle cell culture material at BSL-1 containment practices.
5. Handle harvest material at BSL-1 containment practices.
6. Keep work surfaces clear of unneeded materials.
7. Disinfect the work surface after completing the work.
8. Remove personal protective equipment in the designated area.
9. Wash hands after removing personal protective equipment.
10. Report a containment concern to the area supervisor.

### 4.2 Decontaminate and dispose of biohazard waste

1. Identify biohazard waste at the point of generation.
2. Place spent culture material in a designated biohazard waste container.
3. Place contaminated disposables in a designated biohazard waste container.
4. Place bank-contact items in a designated biohazard waste container.
5. Place harvest-contact items in a designated biohazard waste container.
6. Close the filled container.
7. Label the closed container for autoclave decontamination.
8. Transfer the container to the designated autoclave area.
9. Load the container into the autoclave.
10. Place a chemical indicator in each autoclave load.
11. Start the validated decontamination cycle.
12. Remove the load after the cycle completes.
13. Check the chemical indicator result.
14. Record the chemical indicator result.
15. Escalate a failed chemical indicator result to the area supervisor.
16. Send decontaminated waste for disposal through the designated waste stream.
17. Perform biological-indicator testing of the kill cycle weekly.
18. Record the biological-indicator test result.
19. Escalate a failed biological-indicator result to Quality Assurance.
20. Handle an escalated failure under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
21. Consider corrective and preventive action under SOP-QA-011, Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA).

### 4.3 Respond to a spill

1. Stop work in the affected area.
2. Alert personnel in the immediate area.
3. Assess whether the spill can be managed safely.
4. Retrieve the spill kit from the Quality Control laboratory or the production suite.
5. Wear personal protective equipment from the spill kit.
6. Contain the spill with absorbent material from the spill kit.
7. Clean a spill inside containment as trained personnel of the area.
8. Collect the used absorbent material.
9. Place the collected material in a designated biohazard waste container.
10. Disinfect the affected surface.
11. Remove personal protective equipment.
12. Place used personal protective equipment in a designated biohazard waste container.
13. Wash hands after completing the cleanup.
14. Report the completed cleanup to the area supervisor.
15. Escalate a spill outside containment to the area supervisor the same business day.
16. Escalate a spill outside containment to Quality Assurance the same business day.
17. Escalate any spill that cannot be managed safely to the area supervisor the same business day.
18. Escalate any spill that cannot be managed safely to Quality Assurance the same business day.
19. Handle an escalated spill under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations where product or personnel impact is possible.
20. Clean the affected area under SOP-QA-034, Routine Facility Cleaning, Sanitization, and Sporicidal Rotation after the spill cleanup is complete.

### 4.4 Report personnel exposure

1. Stop the work that led to the exposure.
2. Move away from the affected area.
3. Wash the affected skin area with water.
4. Flush an affected eye with water.
5. Report the exposure to the supervisor the same business day.
6. Describe the material involved in the exposure.
7. Describe the route of exposure.
8. Record the time of the exposure.
9. Complete the exposure report form.
10. Submit the completed report to Quality Assurance.
11. Handle a report with possible product impact under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
12. Consider corrective and preventive action under SOP-QA-011, Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA).

## 5. Records

Records generated under this procedure include autoclave load records with chemical indicator results, weekly biological-indicator test results, spill reports, spill cleanup records, and personnel exposure reports. Each record identifies the material involved, the area, the date, and the personnel who performed the work. Quality Assurance reviews escalated spill records and personnel exposure reports. Completed records are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
