# SOP-QC-012 — Glassware Washing, Depyrogenation, and Endotoxin-Free Consumables

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Control

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how laboratory glassware is washed, how glassware
used for endotoxin testing is depyrogenated, and how endotoxin-free
consumables are received, stored, and used in the Quality Control laboratory.

## 2. Scope

Applies to laboratory glassware washing, depyrogenation of glassware used
for endotoxin testing, and the receipt, storage, and use of certified
endotoxin-free consumables at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics
organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United
States, in the following functions: Quality Control laboratory, Quality
Assurance.

Does not cover cleaning of product-contact equipment or cleaning validation
of product-contact equipment.
Product-contact equipment cleaning validation is governed by
SOP-QA-017, Cleaning Validation; this procedure covers laboratory glassware
and consumables only.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Analyst**

- Wash laboratory glassware in the laboratory washer.
- Depyrogenate glassware used for endotoxin testing by dry heat.
- Inspect washed glassware visually after washing.
- Review the holding period for prepared items before use.
- Use prepared items within the stated holding period.

**Laboratory Supervisor**

- Oversee washing and depyrogenation activities in the laboratory.
- Review the laboratory's glassware record in FL-EDMS.
- Confirm that glassware failing visual inspection is rewashed or removed
  from service.
- Confirm that prepared items are stored covered and protected from
  recontamination.

**Quality Assurance**

- Provide quality oversight of this procedure.
- Oversee qualification of the laboratory washer and the depyrogenation
  oven under SOP-QA-026, Equipment, Facilities, Utilities, and Systems
  Qualification.
- Oversee investigation of an endotoxin result suspected to arise from
  glassware or consumables under SOP-QA-012, Out-of-Specification (OOS)
  Investigation.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Wash glassware

1. Collect the laboratory glassware to be washed.
2. Place the glassware in the laboratory washer.
3. Select the qualified cycle for the laboratory washer.
4. Start the laboratory washer.
5. Perform the final rinsing using water for injection from the system
   operated under SOP-QC-009, Pharmaceutical Water and Clean Steam
   Operations.
6. Unload the glassware after the cycle is complete.
7. Inspect each washed item visually.
8. Rewash any glassware that fails visual inspection after washing.
9. Remove from service any glassware that is not rewashed after failing
   visual inspection.
10. Record the washing in the laboratory's glassware record in FL-EDMS.

### 4.2 Depyrogenate glassware for endotoxin testing

1. Identify the glassware used for endotoxin testing.
2. Wash the identified glassware as described in Section 4.1.
3. Place the washed glassware in the depyrogenation oven.
4. Select the qualified dry heat cycle recorded in FL-EDMS.
5. Start the depyrogenation oven.
6. Unload the glassware after the cycle is complete.
7. Cover the depyrogenated glassware after unloading.
8. Protect the depyrogenated glassware from recontamination after
   unloading.
9. Record the depyrogenation in the laboratory's glassware record in
   FL-EDMS.
10. Refer any endotoxin result suspected to arise from glassware or
    consumables for investigation under SOP-QA-012, Out-of-Specification
    (OOS) Investigation.

### 4.3 Receive endotoxin-free consumables

1. Receive the certified endotoxin-free consumables on delivery.
2. Confirm that the delivered items are the certified endotoxin-free
   consumables purchased as such.
3. Obtain the certificate for the delivered consumables.
4. Provide the certificate to Quality Control for review on receipt.
5. Complete the Quality Control review of the certificate on receipt.
6. Place the received consumables in storage after the certificate review.
7. Record the receipt in FL-EDMS.

### 4.4 Store and issue prepared items

1. Store washed items covered.
2. Store depyrogenated items covered.
3. Protect stored items from recontamination.
4. Check the holding period stated in the laboratory's glassware record in
   FL-EDMS before issuing an item.
5. Issue a prepared item only within its stated holding period.
6. Use each prepared item within its stated holding period.
7. Remove from available stock any item that is no longer within its
   stated holding period.
8. Record the issue of a prepared item in the laboratory's glassware
   record in FL-EDMS.

## 5. Records

Records generated under this procedure include the laboratory's glassware
record in FL-EDMS, records of washing and depyrogenation on the qualified
cycles, and records of the Quality Control review of certificates for
certified endotoxin-free consumables on receipt.
These records are completed and maintained in FL-EDMS, the company EDMS.
All records generated under this procedure are retained under the retention
requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
