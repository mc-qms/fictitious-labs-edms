# SOP-QA-013 — Complaint Handling

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

Establish how complaints about a product are recorded, reviewed, investigated, and answered, and how they are assessed for escalation to recall or regulatory reporting.

## 2. Scope

Applies to every complaint received about FL-101 at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Assurance, Quality Control laboratory, Manufacturing.

Does not cover adverse-event intake and reporting for pharmacovigilance, which follows SOP-RA-001, Adverse Event Intake and Reporting, or commercial complaints with no quality content.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Complaint Intake** (Quality Assurance)
  - Receive complaints.
  - Document oral complaints when received.
  - Create or update the complaint record.

- **Investigator**
  - Conduct assigned investigations.
  - Own the investigation record, including findings, conclusions, and follow-up.

- **Quality Assurance**
  - Review complaints that may involve a product specification failure.
  - Determine whether investigation is needed.
  - Oversee complaint assessment and records.

- **Regulatory Affairs**
  - Assess potential regulatory reporting and recall escalation with Quality Assurance.
  - Coordinate regulatory communications.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Receive and record the complaint

- Record each written or oral complaint in a designated complaint file.
- For each complaint, record the product name and strength, lot number, complainant's name, nature of the complaint, and any reply to the complainant, when known.
- The complaint file is the complaint record in FL-EDMS. Any employee who receives a complaint — by letter, email, telephone, or in person — forwards it to Quality Assurance the same business day.
- The person who receives an oral complaint documents it when received.
- Quality Assurance (Complaint Intake) enters the complaint in FL-EDMS within 1 business day of receipt.
- Quality Assurance (Complaint Intake) assigns the complaint number (format COMP-<year>-<number>, e.g., COMP-2026-001).
- Quality Assurance reviews each complaint within 5 business days of receipt.
- A reply is sent to the complainant within 30 calendar days of receipt, at closure of the complaint where an investigation is performed.

### 4.2 Review and assess the complaint

- Have Quality Assurance review complaints that may involve a product failing to meet a specification.
- Quality Assurance determines whether an investigation is needed. An investigation is required when the complaint may involve a product failing to meet a specification, a product quality defect, or a mix-up or labeling error affecting distributed product.
- Quality Assurance documents the determination and its basis in the complaint record.
- For a possible specification failure, determine whether an investigation under 21 CFR § 211.192 is needed.
- Review each complaint to assess whether it may involve an adverse experience that must be reported to FDA under 21 CFR 600.80.
- A complaint that may involve an adverse experience is handed off the same business day to Regulatory Affairs under SOP-RA-001, Adverse Event Intake and Reporting.
- The handoff — date, information transferred, and recipient — is documented in the complaint record.
- The handoff is tracked in FL-EDMS.
- The QA Manager, with Regulatory Affairs, makes the regulatory reporting determination under 21 CFR 600.80. The complaint investigation under this procedure continues in parallel where the complaint also alleges a quality defect.
- A confirmed quality defect that could affect the safety or quality of distributed product is the recall escalation trigger. The QA Manager decides whether a recall assessment proceeds.
- The assessment, its conclusion, and its rationale are documented in the complaint record.
- Any recall is executed under SOP-QA-014, Product Recall.

### 4.3 Investigate

- When an investigation is needed, the Investigator assigned to the complaint must conduct it.
- The Investigator documents the findings and follow-up.
- Quality Assurance assigns the Investigator — from the function that owns the activity the complaint concerns.
- Quality Assurance records the assignment in the complaint record.
- The investigation examines the complaint information, the batch and distribution records for the lot concerned, retained samples or returned product where available and appropriate, and related complaints, deviations, and out-of-specification results. The investigation is completed within 30 calendar days of assignment.
- Quality Assurance reviews the investigation record before the complaint is closed.
- Quality Assurance approves the investigation record before the complaint is closed.
- Include the investigation record, or a copy, in the complaint record.
- Keep the investigation record or copy at the establishment where the investigation occurred, in accordance with 21 CFR § 211.180(c).
- If no investigation is conducted, document why it was considered unnecessary.
- If no investigation is conducted, identify the person responsible for that determination.

### 4.4 Respond and close the complaint

- Record any reply to the complainant in the complaint record.
- Quality Assurance prepares the reply.
- The QA Manager reviews the reply when the complaint involves a confirmed quality defect or a possible adverse experience.
- The QA Manager approves the reply when the complaint involves a confirmed quality defect or a possible adverse experience.
- Quality Assurance sends the reply.
- Quality Assurance closes the complaint when the investigation is complete and approved (or the reason no investigation was needed is documented), the reply has been sent, any adverse-experience handoff is documented, and a CAPA determination has been made under SOP-QA-011, Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA).
- The QA Manager approves closure when a quality defect is confirmed.

### 4.5 Review complaint information

- Review complaint information as part of product quality evaluation.
- Include review of complaints and investigations in the product quality evaluation.
- Include complaint-process performance in pharmaceutical quality system review. Complaint-process performance indicators may be considered as part of that review.
- Quality Assurance reviews complaint information quarterly: complaint counts by type and lot, repeat complaints, investigation timeliness, and any adverse trends.
- The review is recorded in FL-EDMS and is an input to management review.
- Adverse trends are escalated to the QA Manager.
- Adverse trends are assessed for CAPA under SOP-QA-011.

## 5. Records

Complaint records (COMP-<year>-<number>) in FL-EDMS, the file designated for product complaints, including the product name and strength, lot number, complainant's name, nature of the complaint, and any reply to the complainant; where an investigation is conducted, its findings, conclusions, and follow-up; and where no investigation is conducted, the reason it was considered unnecessary and the name of the person responsible for that determination. The investigation record, or a copy, is kept at the establishment where the investigation occurred, in accordance with 21 CFR § 211.180(c). Complaint records are retained until at least 1 year after the product's expiration date or 1 year after receipt of the complaint, whichever is longer, under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
