# WI-MF-005 — Physical Quarantine, Reject Cage, and Status Stickers

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Manufacturing

## 1. Purpose

This instruction describes how to apply and change status stickers, how to use the reject cage, and how to resolve a disagreement between a sticker and the status in FL-EDMS. It sits under SOP-QA-023, Warehouse and Distribution Control, and batch status is controlled by Quality Assurance under SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release.

## 2. Apply and change status stickers

1. Assign the status Quarantine to a material on receipt, as Warehouse.
2. Apply a yellow sticker to an item with the status Quarantine.
3. Apply an orange sticker to an item with the status Hold.
4. Apply a green sticker to an item with the status Released.
5. Apply a red sticker to an item with the status Rejected.
6. Check that the colour of the sticker matches the status of the item.
7. Change the status of a batch only as Quality Assurance.
8. Change the status of a material only as Quality Assurance or Quality Control.
9. Do not change the status of a batch as Warehouse.
10. Do not change the status of a material as Warehouse.
11. Release a material for use only as Quality Control.
12. Replace the sticker on an item when its status is changed by Quality Assurance or Quality Control.
13. Apply the sticker for the new status when a status is changed.
14. Remove the sticker for the previous status when a status is changed.
15. Check the status of an item in FL-EDMS before replacing a missing sticker.
16. Check the status of an item in FL-EDMS before replacing a damaged sticker.
17. Replace a missing sticker only after the status is confirmed in FL-EDMS.
18. Replace a damaged sticker only after the status is confirmed in FL-EDMS.
19. Apply a replacement sticker in the colour for the status confirmed in FL-EDMS.
20. Record sticker application and replacement in accordance with SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.

## 3. Use the reject cage

1. Place rejected materials in the reject cage.
2. Place rejected product in the reject cage.
3. Place recalled materials and product in the designated segregated area.
4. Place returned materials and product in the designated segregated area.
5. Hold rejected materials and product in the designated segregated area.
6. Hold recalled materials and product in the designated segregated area, under SOP-QA-014, Product Recall.
7. Hold returned materials and product in the designated segregated area.
8. Keep the reject cage locked.
9. Allow access to the reject cage only to Quality Assurance.
10. Allow access to the reject cage only to the Warehouse Supervisor, in addition to Quality Assurance.
11. Do not allow access to the reject cage to any other person.
12. Apply a red sticker to an item with the status Rejected before it is placed in the reject cage.
13. Lock the reject cage after an item is placed in it.
14. Lock the reject cage after an item is removed from it by Quality Assurance or the Warehouse Supervisor.

## 4. Resolve a sticker/status disagreement

1. Compare the status on the sticker with the status in FL-EDMS.
2. Stop work on the item where the sticker and the FL-EDMS status disagree.
3. Notify Quality Assurance where the sticker and the FL-EDMS status disagree.
4. Do not change the FL-EDMS status as Warehouse.
5. Leave the FL-EDMS status unchanged until Quality Assurance resolves the disagreement.
6. Do not resume work on the item until Quality Assurance resolves the disagreement.
7. Raise a deviation for the disagreement under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations, where Quality Assurance requires it.
8. Record the disagreement and its resolution in accordance with SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.

## 5. Control of this instruction

1. Quality Assurance and the Manufacturing Supervisor approve this instruction and each revision of it.
2. Make each revision through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
