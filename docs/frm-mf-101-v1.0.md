# FRM-MF-101 — Master Batch Record, FL-101 Drug Substance

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Manufacturing

This is the master batch record for FL-101 drug substance, approved and issued under SOP-QA-027, Batch Record Content and Control. Quality Assurance reproduces this master exactly to create the executed batch record for each batch; the executed record carries the batch number and page numbers (page X of Y) printed on every page.

## Manufacturing steps

### Step 1 — Inoculation

Thaw one vial of WCB-FL101 and inoculate the seed culture. Record the WCB vial lot number, the date performed, and your initials.

### Step 2 — Cell culture

Culture in the 200 L bioreactor (BIO-01) for 12 days at 36.5 °C ± 0.5 °C. Record the culture pH on day 12, before harvest. Acceptable pH range: **6.8–7.4**. Record the date performed and your initials.

### Step 3 — Harvest

Harvest the culture and record the harvest volume in litres. Record the date performed and your initials.

### Step 4 — Purification

Purify the harvest by capture chromatography (RES-310) and record the pool volume in litres. Record the date performed and your initials.

### Step 5 — Sterile filtration and bulk fill

Filter the pool through a sterilizing filter (FLT-022) and fill into 500 mL bulk containers. Record the number of containers filled and the total filled volume. Enter the final protein concentration result, tested under TM-QC-101, Protein Concentration by UV Absorbance (A280).

## Batch summary

Complete the batch summary at the close of manufacture: batch number; final protein concentration; total filled volume; actual yield and percentage of theoretical yield; and the retest date assigned under SOP-QC-002, Stability Testing.

## Label specimen

Attach a specimen of the labeling applied to the batch containers. Label content follows WI-MF-001, Packaging and Labeling Instructions, FL-101 Drug Substance: lot number, actual protein concentration, container volume, and retest date are taken from this batch record.

## Batch record list

The completed batch record for a batch consists of: this executed batch record; the laboratory records for every test in SPEC-QC-101, FL-101 Drug Substance Specification; the labeling control records under SOP-QA-022, Labeling and Packaging Control; and any investigation or sampling records associated with the batch.

## Completion rules

Make every entry at the time the step is performed, following SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices. Initial and date each step you perform. No required entry may be left blank. Review and release of the completed record follow SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release.
