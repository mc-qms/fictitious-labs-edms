# SOP-QA-032 — Single-Use Systems and Extractables/Leachables

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines how single-use systems in product contact are qualified, received, released, and controlled at Fictitious Labs Inc. It also defines how extractables and leachables risk is assessed for those systems before use and when changes occur.

## 2. Scope

Applies to single-use components in product contact for FL-101 manufacture at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Assurance, Quality Control laboratory, Manufacturing. FL-101 is a monoclonal antibody drug substance. The components in scope are bioreactor and transfer bags, tubing sets, sterile connectors, and single-use filter assemblies.

Does not cover sterilizing-grade filters themselves, which are governed by SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills; this procedure governs the single-use assemblies around them. Sterilizing-grade filters remain governed by SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills, including the 0.2 µm maximum nominal pore size and integrity testing after use and before use after sterilization where the contamination control strategy requires it.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Quality Assurance**

- Approves each extractables/leachables risk assessment before first use of an assembly.
- Approves the qualification of a single-use assembly for use.
- Approves changes to single-use components through change control.
- Dispositions deviations associated with single-use systems.

**QA Manager**

- Reviews qualification documentation for completeness.
- Reviews extractables/leachables impact assessments for changes.
- Escalates unresolved supplier or assessment issues to Quality Assurance.

**Quality Control**

- Reviews each lot's certificates before the lot is released for use.
- Records the outcome of each certificate review.
- Notifies Quality Assurance of a certificate that does not conform.

**Laboratory Supervisor**

- Assigns certificate reviews to Quality Control personnel.
- Verifies that certificate reviews are completed before release.
- Retains laboratory review records for retrieval.

**Manufacturing Supervisor**

- Confirms that only released lots are used in FL-101 manufacture.
- Identifies each single-use assembly used in a batch.
- Reports damage to a single-use component to Quality Assurance.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Qualify a single-use assembly for use

1. Identify each single-use component in the proposed assembly.
2. Confirm that each component is in product contact for FL-101 manufacture.
3. Verify that each component supplier appears on the Approved Supplier List under SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality.
4. Request the supplier's extractables data for each component.
5. Prepare an extractables/leachables risk assessment for the assembly under SOP-QA-009, Quality Risk Management.
6. Submit the risk assessment to Quality Assurance for approval.
7. Record the approved qualification in the assembly qualification record.
8. Release the assembly for first use only after Quality Assurance approval.

### 4.2 Receive and release single-use components

1. Receive each lot of single-use components into inventory.
2. Verify the lot identity against the delivery documentation.
3. Segregate the lot pending Quality Control review.
4. Obtain the certificates for the lot.
5. Review the certificate of sterility for the lot, as applicable.
6. Review the certificate of endotoxin conformity for the lot, as applicable.
7. Review the certificate of particulate conformity for the lot, as applicable.
8. Record the certificate review outcome.
9. Release the lot for use after a conforming certificate review.
10. Quarantine a lot with a nonconforming certificate.
11. Notify Quality Assurance of a nonconforming certificate.
12. Handle a nonconforming lot under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations, where Quality Assurance directs it.

### 4.3 Assess extractables and leachables

1. Identify each product-contact material in the single-use assembly.
2. Obtain the supplier's extractables data for each material.
3. Document the source of each set of extractables data.
4. Assess the extractables data under SOP-QA-009, Quality Risk Management.
5. Identify leachables of concern for FL-101 from the assessment.
6. Document the rationale for each leachables conclusion.
7. Define any follow-up testing identified by the assessment.
8. Submit the completed assessment to Quality Assurance.
9. Obtain Quality Assurance approval of the assessment before first use.
10. File the approved assessment with the assembly qualification record.
11. Reassess the assembly when new supplier extractables data become available.
12. Record the outcome of each reassessment.

### 4.4 Control changes to single-use components

1. Identify a proposed change to a single-use component.
2. Identify a proposed change of supplier for a single-use component.
3. Initiate the change through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
4. Prepare an extractables/leachables impact assessment for the change.
5. Verify that a new supplier appears on the Approved Supplier List under SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality.
6. Submit the impact assessment to Quality Assurance for review.
7. Obtain Quality Assurance approval before implementing the change.
8. Update the assembly qualification record after approval.
9. Update the extractables/leachables risk assessment after approval.
10. Communicate the approved change to Manufacturing.
11. Communicate the approved change to Quality Control.
12. Close the change only after each update is complete.

## 5. Records

Records generated under this procedure include assembly qualification records, extractables/leachables risk assessments, extractables/leachables impact assessments, lot certificate reviews, and lot release records. Each record identifies the assembly or lot it covers and the person who completed the activity. Records are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
