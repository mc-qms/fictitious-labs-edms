# SOP-QA-043 — HVAC, HEPA Integrity, and Differential Pressure Excursion Response

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how HEPA filters serving classified areas are integrity-tested, how differential pressures are monitored, and how pressure excursions are responded to and assessed. It supports continued control of classified areas in service.

## 2. Scope

Applies to HVAC systems, HEPA filters, and differential pressure monitoring for classified areas at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Assurance, Engineering and Maintenance, Manufacturing, Quality Control laboratory. Classified areas are Grade B, C, and D with Grade A critical zones.

Does not cover facility and HVAC qualification itself, which is governed by SOP-QA-026, Equipment, Facilities, Utilities, and Systems Qualification, or environmental monitoring, which is governed by SOP-QC-001, Environmental Monitoring. This procedure covers periodic HEPA integrity testing, pressure monitoring, and excursion response in service.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Maintenance Technician**

- Perform HEPA integrity testing as assigned.
- Remove a failed HEPA filter from service as directed.
- Record test and maintenance results.

**Equipment Owner**

- Maintain the schedule for HEPA integrity testing for assigned systems.
- Arrange testing by Engineering and Maintenance or by a qualified contractor.
- Confirm a failed filter remains out of service until it is repaired or replaced and re-tested.

**Manufacturing Operator**

- Monitor differential pressure alarms in the manufacturing area during production.
- Respond to a pressure alarm or reversal as described in this procedure.
- Pause production in the affected area where the excursion is sustained, as directed.

**Manufacturing Supervisor**

- Respond with the operator to a pressure alarm or reversal.
- Confirm production in the affected area is paused where the excursion is sustained.
- Notify Quality Assurance of a pressure excursion.

**Quality Assurance**

- Approve HEPA integrity test results.
- Assess product impact for a pressure excursion together with data under SOP-QC-001, Environmental Monitoring.
- Authorize resumption of production after a pressure excursion.
- Handle excursions under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

**QA Manager**

- Review excursion assessments that Quality Assurance escalates.
- Approve return to service where Quality Assurance escalates the decision.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Integrity-test HEPA filters

1. Confirm the HEPA filter is due for integrity testing.
2. Identify the filter and the classified area it serves.
3. Test HEPA filters serving classified areas at installation.
4. Test HEPA filters after each filter change.
5. Test HEPA filters at least every 12 months for Grade A/B areas.
6. Test HEPA filters at least every 24 months for Grade C/D areas.
7. Perform integrity testing by aerosol leak testing using PAO.
8. Assign testing to Engineering and Maintenance or to a qualified contractor.
9. Confirm a contractor performing testing is a supplier under SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality.
10. Record the integrity test result for each filter tested.
11. Submit the integrity test results to Quality Assurance for approval.
12. Remove a failed filter from service.
13. Repair or replace the failed filter.
14. Re-test the repaired or replaced filter.
15. Return the filter to service only after the re-test is complete and the results are approved by Quality Assurance.

### 4.2 Monitor differential pressures

1. Monitor differential pressures continuously using the building monitoring system.
2. Compare monitored pressures against the limits established in each area's qualification under SOP-QA-026, Equipment, Facilities, Utilities, and Systems Qualification.
3. Respond to alarms generated by the building monitoring system.
4. Monitor temperature continuously against the values established in each area's qualification.
5. Monitor relative humidity continuously against the values established in each area's qualification.
6. Monitor temperature and relative humidity per SOP-QC-001, Environmental Monitoring.
7. Perform airflow visualization (smoke) studies at qualification under SOP-QA-026, Equipment, Facilities, Utilities, and Systems Qualification.
8. Perform airflow visualization (smoke) studies after a significant change under SOP-QA-026, Equipment, Facilities, Utilities, and Systems Qualification.

### 4.3 Respond to a pressure excursion

1. Note the pressure alarm or reversal when it occurs.
2. Record the pressure alarm or reversal.
3. Notify the Manufacturing Supervisor of the alarm or reversal.
4. Respond to the affected area as the operator and Manufacturing Supervisor.
5. Pause production in the affected area where the excursion is sustained.
6. Notify Quality Assurance of the excursion.
7. Record the area affected by the excursion.
8. Record whether production in the affected area was paused.
9. Assess product impact together with data under SOP-QC-001, Environmental Monitoring.
10. Handle the excursion under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

### 4.4 Return an area to service

1. Confirm the pressure excursion has been assessed by Quality Assurance.
2. Confirm product impact has been assessed together with data under SOP-QC-001, Environmental Monitoring.
3. Obtain Quality Assurance authorization before resuming production.
4. Resume production in the affected area only after Quality Assurance authorization.
5. Confirm a HEPA filter removed from service for a failed test has been repaired or replaced.
6. Confirm the repaired or replaced HEPA filter has been re-tested.
7. Confirm Quality Assurance has approved the re-test results before returning the filter to service.

## 5. Records

Records generated under this procedure include HEPA integrity test results and Quality Assurance approvals, differential pressure monitoring and alarm records from the building monitoring system, airflow visualization study records, pressure excursion records and product impact assessments, deviation records raised under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations, and authorizations to resume production and return an area or filter to service. Records are completed and retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
