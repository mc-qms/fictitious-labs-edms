# CCS-001 — Contamination Control Strategy

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Manufacturing

## 1. Purpose

State the contamination control strategy for aseptic manufacture of FL-101 drug substance at Fictitious Labs Inc.: the controls over facility and process design, personnel, materials, utilities, and monitoring that together prevent microbial, particulate, and endotoxin contamination. This strategy is maintained under SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills, which assigns its oversight to Quality Assurance.

## 2. Basis

Sterility is built into the process; it is not created by testing. Contamination risks are identified, evaluated, and controlled by quality risk management under SOP-QA-009, Quality Risk Management, considering personnel, materials, equipment, utilities, premises, and process activities as sources of microbial, particulate, and endotoxin contamination.

## 3. Strategy elements

### 3.1 Facility and process design

- Aseptic operations are performed in Grade A critical zones with a Grade B background, as designated in FL-EDMS and governed by SOP-QA-024.
- Personnel and material flows are unidirectional where the facility design permits, and airlocks separate areas of different grades.
- Open manipulations are minimized by process design; closed processing is used wherever the process allows.

### 3.2 Personnel

- Only personnel qualified in aseptic technique and gowning under SOP-QA-024 and SOP-QA-005, Training and Qualification, enter Grade A and Grade B areas.
- Gowning is assessed against the pass criteria in SOP-QA-024, with monitoring under SOP-QC-001, Environmental Monitoring.

### 3.3 Cleaning and disinfection

- Cleaning and disinfection of graded areas follow the validated program referenced in SOP-QA-024, with disinfectant effectiveness established under SOP-QA-017, Cleaning Validation.
- Cleaning status and hold times are recorded in the batch record.

### 3.4 Sterilizing filtration

- FL-101 bulk is sterilized by filtration through a 0.2 µm sterilizing-grade filter under SOP-QA-024, with filter integrity testing before and after filtration.
- Bioburden is controlled to the limit in SPEC-QC-101 before filtration.

### 3.5 Environmental and process monitoring

- Viable and non-viable monitoring of graded areas is performed under SOP-QC-001, Environmental Monitoring, against the alert and action levels approved by Quality Assurance in FL-EDMS.
- Aseptic process simulations are performed under SOP-QA-024 to demonstrate that the process, personnel, and interventions together maintain sterility.

### 3.6 Utilities and equipment

- Air handling, water systems, and process equipment are maintained under qualification, monitoring, and planned maintenance under SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance, and SOP-QA-026, Equipment, Facilities, Utilities, and Systems Qualification.
- Equipment cleaning is validated under SOP-QA-017, Cleaning Validation.

## 4. Control of this strategy

1. Quality Assurance approves this strategy and each revision of it.
2. Review this strategy at least annually, as part of the review assigned by SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills.
3. Review this strategy additionally whenever any of the following occurs:
   - An aseptic process simulation failure.
   - An adverse environmental monitoring trend.
   - A change to the facility, equipment, process, or personnel model.
4. Make each revision through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
5. Include, in every revision, each element of section 3 of this strategy; an element may be marked not applicable only with a documented justification approved by Quality Assurance.

## 5. Records

This strategy, its approvals, and its review records, retained in FL-EDMS under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
