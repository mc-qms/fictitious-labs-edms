# SOP-QA-042 — Chromatography Resin and Ultrafiltration Membrane Lifecycle Management

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Manufacturing

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how chromatography resins and ultrafiltration membranes used in FL-101 purification are placed into service, monitored, stored, and removed from service.
It ensures that each resin lot and membrane set is used only within its approved maximum reuse cycles.

## 2. Scope

Applies to chromatography resin lots and ultrafiltration membrane sets used in FL-101 purification at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Manufacturing, Quality Assurance, Quality Control laboratory.

Does not cover process validation itself, which follows SOP-QA-016, Process Validation; this procedure manages the in-service lifecycle of resins and membranes whose reuse limits validation establishes.
Does not cover the manufacture of FL-101 itself, which is recorded in FRM-MF-101, Master Batch Record, FL-101 Drug Substance.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Manufacturing Operator**

- Place resins and membranes into service as described in Section 4.1.
- Sanitize and store resins and membranes as described in Section 4.2.
- Record the cycle count for each use in FRM-MF-101, Master Batch Record, FL-101 Drug Substance.
- Remove a resin or membrane from service when directed under Section 4.4.

**Manufacturing Supervisor**

- Verify that a lifecycle record exists in FL-EDMS before a resin or membrane is placed into service.
- Verify that cycle counts recorded in the batch record match the lifecycle record.
- Confirm that performance testing is scheduled as described in Section 4.3.
- Notify Quality Assurance when a performance failure occurs.

**Quality Assurance**

- Approve the maximum reuse cycles stated in each lifecycle record.
- Assess batches made since the last acceptable performance result under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations, when a performance failure occurs.
- Approve any lifecycle extension through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
- Approve removal from service where Section 4.4 requires it.

**Quality Control**

- Perform performance testing support where the lifecycle record assigns it.
- Perform integrity testing support where the lifecycle record assigns it.
- Report performance and integrity test results to Manufacturing and Quality Assurance.
- Record test results as stated in the lifecycle record.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Place a resin or membrane into service

1. Identify the chromatography resin lot or ultrafiltration membrane set to be used in FL-101 purification.
2. Locate the lifecycle record for that resin lot or membrane set in FL-EDMS.
3. Confirm that the lifecycle record states the identity of the resin lot or membrane set.
4. Confirm that the lifecycle record states the first-use date.
5. Confirm that the lifecycle record states the current cycle count.
6. Confirm that the lifecycle record states the sanitization and storage conditions.
7. Confirm that the lifecycle record states the maximum reuse cycles.
8. Confirm that the maximum reuse cycles were set from validation under SOP-QA-016, Process Validation.
9. Confirm that the maximum reuse cycles were approved by Quality Assurance.
10. For a membrane set, integrity-test the membrane by pressure hold before first use.
11. Record the integrity test result in the lifecycle record.
12. For a resin lot, measure column performance at first use.
13. Measure HETP as part of the column performance measurement.
14. Measure asymmetry as part of the column performance measurement.
15. Compare the HETP and asymmetry results against the criteria in the lifecycle record.
16. Notify Quality Assurance if a result does not meet the criteria in the lifecycle record.
17. Place the resin or membrane into service only after the checks in this section are complete.

### 4.2 Sanitize and store between campaigns

1. Remove the resin or membrane from the process at the end of the campaign.
2. Review the sanitization conditions stated in the lifecycle record.
3. Sanitize the resin using the conditions stated in the lifecycle record.
4. Store the resin in the storage solution stated in the lifecycle record.
5. Review the storage conditions stated in the lifecycle record for the membrane set.
6. Store the membrane set under the conditions stated in its lifecycle record.
7. Record the sanitization performed in the lifecycle record.
8. Record the storage conditions used in the lifecycle record.
9. Record the storage start date in the lifecycle record.
10. Integrity-test a membrane by pressure hold after any storage period.
11. Record the integrity test result in the lifecycle record.
12. Notify Quality Assurance if an integrity test result does not meet the criteria in the lifecycle record.
13. Return the resin or membrane to service only after the steps in this section are complete.

### 4.3 Monitor performance over the lifecycle

1. Review the cycle count in the lifecycle record before each use.
2. Record the cycle count for each use in FRM-MF-101, Master Batch Record, FL-101 Drug Substance.
3. Update the cycle count in the lifecycle record after each use.
4. Measure column performance at first use.
5. Measure column performance at least every 10 cycles.
6. Measure HETP as part of each column performance measurement.
7. Measure asymmetry as part of each column performance measurement.
8. Perform performance testing with Quality Control support where the lifecycle record assigns it.
9. Compare each HETP and asymmetry result against the criteria in the lifecycle record.
10. Record each performance result in the lifecycle record.
11. Compare the current cycle count against the maximum reuse cycles in the lifecycle record before each use.
12. Notify the Manufacturing Supervisor when the cycle count reaches the maximum reuse cycles.
13. Notify Quality Assurance when a performance result does not meet the criteria in the lifecycle record.
14. Stop further use of the resin or membrane after a performance failure until Section 4.4 is completed.

### 4.4 Remove from service or extend the lifecycle

1. Remove the resin or membrane from service when a performance failure occurs.
2. Identify the resin lot or membrane set removed from service in its lifecycle record.
3. Record the date of removal in the lifecycle record.
4. Record the reason for removal in the lifecycle record.
5. Notify Quality Assurance of the performance failure.
6. Provide the date of the last acceptable performance result to Quality Assurance.
7. Support Quality Assurance in assessing batches made since the last acceptable result under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
8. Remove the resin or membrane from service when its cycle count reaches the maximum reuse cycles.
9. Do not use a resin or membrane beyond its approved maximum reuse cycles without an approved extension.
10. Raise a change through SOP-QA-004, Change Control, to request a lifecycle extension beyond the approved maximum.
11. State the requested extension in the change record.
12. Obtain Quality Assurance approval for the extension before any further use.
13. Update the lifecycle record with the approved maximum reuse cycles after an approved extension.
14. Record the approved extension in the lifecycle record.
15. Close the lifecycle record when the resin or membrane is permanently removed from service.

## 5. Records

Lifecycle records for each chromatography resin lot and ultrafiltration membrane set are maintained in FL-EDMS and state the identity, first-use date, cycle count, sanitization and storage conditions, and maximum reuse cycles for that resin lot or membrane set.
Cycle counts for each use are recorded in FRM-MF-101, Master Batch Record, FL-101 Drug Substance.
Performance results, integrity test results, removal records, and approved lifecycle extensions are recorded in the lifecycle record for the resin lot or membrane set concerned.
All records generated under this procedure are completed and maintained in accordance with SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices, and are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
