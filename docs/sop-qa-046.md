# SOP-QA-046 — Compendial Surveillance

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how Fictitious Labs Inc. monitors public pharmacopeial sources for compendial changes and assesses and implements adopted changes. It ensures that methods, specifications, and procedures in use remain aligned with adopted compendial requirements by their official dates.

## 2. Scope

Applies to compendial surveillance of public pharmacopeial sources and to the assessment and implementation of adopted compendial changes affecting methods, specifications, and procedures at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Assurance, Regulatory Affairs, Quality Control laboratory.

Does not cover the determination of whether a change affects a regulatory filing or established condition; that determination is assessed under SOP-QA-004, Change Control (§4.3), not under this procedure. Does not cover specification changes themselves, which are governed by SOP-QA-028, Specification Management.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Regulatory Affairs**

- Performs the monitoring of public pharmacopeial sources under this procedure.
- Reviews the public sources listed in Section 4.1 on a monthly basis.
- Logs each relevant item in the compendial surveillance log in FL-EDMS.
- Notifies Quality Assurance of each relevant item identified.
- Provides the public proposal and notice documents used for assessment.

**Quality Assurance**

- Owns this procedure.
- Coordinates the impact assessment for each adopted compendial change.
- Works with the owning function to complete each impact assessment within the timeframe in Section 4.3.
- Confirms that each adopted change is implemented through SOP-QA-004, Change Control, before its official date.
- Escalates a change with an official date that the site cannot meet to the QA Manager.

**QA Manager**

- Reviews escalations for compendial changes with an official date that the site cannot meet.
- Decides the handling of the gap until the change is implemented.
- Confirms that a gap is handled under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations, where applicable.

**Study Director**

- Supports method impact assessments where assigned.
- Assesses the impact of an adopted compendial change on analytical procedures in use.
- Supports verification activities for a revised compendial procedure, per SOP-QC-003, Analytical Procedure Validation, and SOP-QC-007, Analytical Method Transfer and Compendial Verification, conventions.
- Records the outcome of the method impact assessment for Quality Assurance review.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Monitor the public sources

1. Regulatory Affairs reviews Pharmacopeial Forum proposals monthly.
2. Regulatory Affairs reviews compendial notices monthly.
3. Regulatory Affairs reviews revision bulletins monthly.
4. Regulatory Affairs reviews interim revision announcements monthly.
5. Regulatory Affairs reviews FDA publications monthly.
6. Regulatory Affairs reviews ICH publications monthly.
7. Regulatory Affairs identifies items that are relevant to Fictitious Labs Inc. methods, specifications, or procedures.
8. Regulatory Affairs identifies items that are relevant to SPEC-101, FL-101 Drug Substance Specification.
9. Regulatory Affairs retains the public proposal and notice documents for each relevant item.
10. Regulatory Affairs cites USP chapter numbers and titles only where a citation is needed.
11. Regulatory Affairs does not reproduce USP text in any Fictitious Labs document or assessment.
12. Regulatory Affairs bases each assessment on the public proposal and notice documents.

### 4.2 Log and triage identified changes

1. Regulatory Affairs logs each relevant item in the compendial surveillance log in FL-EDMS.
2. Regulatory Affairs records the source of the item in the log entry.
3. Regulatory Affairs records the date the item was identified in the log entry.
4. Regulatory Affairs records the compendial change described in the item in the log entry.
5. Regulatory Affairs records the official date of the change in the log entry, where stated.
6. Regulatory Affairs records the USP chapter number and title in the log entry, where applicable.
7. Regulatory Affairs notifies Quality Assurance of the log entry.
8. Quality Assurance reviews the log entry with Regulatory Affairs.
9. Quality Assurance identifies the owning function for the affected method, specification, or procedure.
10. Quality Assurance determines whether the change is adopted.
11. Quality Assurance records the triage outcome in the compendial surveillance log.
12. Quality Assurance closes the log entry for an item that is not adopted.
13. Quality Assurance carries forward each adopted change to Section 4.3.

### 4.3 Assess impact and implement

1. Quality Assurance assigns the impact assessment to the owning function.
2. Quality Assurance sets the due date for the impact assessment within 30 calendar days of identification of the adopted change.
3. The owning function assesses the impact of the adopted change on methods in use.
4. The owning function assesses the impact of the adopted change on specifications in use.
5. The owning function assesses the impact of the adopted change on procedures in use.
6. The Study Director assesses the impact on an analytical procedure where assigned.
7. The owning function records the impact assessment in FL-EDMS.
8. Quality Assurance reviews the completed impact assessment.
9. Quality Assurance confirms the implementation actions required for the adopted change.
10. The owning function implements the adopted change through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
11. The owning function completes implementation before the official date of the adopted change.
12. The owning function follows SOP-QC-007, Analytical Method Transfer and Compendial Verification, for verification where an adopted change alters a compendial procedure in use.
13. Quality Assurance verifies that implementation is complete.
14. Quality Assurance updates the compendial surveillance log with the implementation outcome.
15. Quality Assurance escalates the change to the QA Manager where the site cannot meet the official date.
16. Quality Assurance ensures the gap is handled under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations, until the change is implemented.
17. The QA Manager reviews the escalated gap.
18. Quality Assurance closes the log entry after the change is implemented.

## 5. Records

The compendial surveillance log is maintained in FL-EDMS and records each relevant item identified, its source, its date of identification, and its official date where stated. Impact assessments, triage outcomes, and implementation outcomes are recorded in FL-EDMS and linked to the corresponding log entry. Records generated under this procedure are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
