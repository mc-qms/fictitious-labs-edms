# WI-QC-001 — Freezer Alarm and Power Outage Response

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Control

## 1. Purpose

Provide the response to a building monitoring system alarm for storage units holding cell banks, FL-101 drug substance, stability samples, and retention samples, for use under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations, which governs the assessment of the excursion.

## 2. Respond to an alarm

1. Receive the alarm from the building monitoring system.
2. Identify the unit that raised the alarm.
3. Confirm whether the unit is an LN2 freezer.
4. Confirm whether the unit is a stability chamber.
5. Confirm whether the unit is a −20 °C ± 5 °C storage unit.
6. Identify the on-call responder named on the roster maintained by Quality Control.
7. Acknowledge the alarm within 30 minutes.
8. Record the acknowledgement in FL-EDMS.
9. Check the temperature of the alarmed unit.
10. For an LN2 freezer, check the temperature against ≤ −130 °C.
11. For a −20 °C storage unit, check the temperature against −20 °C ± 5 °C.
12. Note that cell banks are stored in vapor-phase liquid nitrogen in the LN2 freezers.
13. Note that FL-101 drug substance is stored in the −20 °C ± 5 °C storage.
14. Note that stability samples are stored in the stability chambers and in the −20 °C ± 5 °C storage.
15. Note that retention samples are stored in the −20 °C ± 5 °C storage.
16. Determine whether the temperature can be restored within the unit's qualified holdover.
17. Notify Quality Assurance the same business day when an excursion has occurred.
18. Assess the excursion under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
19. Follow SOP-QC-004, Cell Bank Management, for a cell bank freezer failure.
20. Follow SOP-QC-002, Stability Testing, for stability storage.
21. Follow SOP-QC-005, Retention (Reserve) Samples, for retention samples.
22. Follow SOP-QA-023, Warehouse and Distribution Control, for warehouse product storage.
23. Record the alarm response in FL-EDMS.
24. Record the response in accordance with SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.

## 3. Move contents to a backup unit

1. Start this section when the temperature cannot be restored within the unit's qualified holdover.
2. Identify the product stored in the affected unit.
3. Identify the samples stored in the affected unit.
4. Select a qualified backup unit.
5. Move the product to the qualified backup unit.
6. Move the samples to the qualified backup unit.
7. Move cell banks to a qualified backup unit for a cell bank freezer failure.
8. Apply SOP-QC-004, Cell Bank Management, to the cell bank transfer.
9. Move stability samples to a qualified backup unit for stability storage.
10. Apply SOP-QC-002, Stability Testing, to the stability sample transfer.
11. Move retention samples to a qualified backup unit.
12. Apply SOP-QC-005, Retention (Reserve) Samples, to the retention sample transfer.
13. Move FL-101 drug substance to a qualified backup unit.
14. Apply SOP-QA-023, Warehouse and Distribution Control, to warehouse product storage.
15. Record the transfer in FL-EDMS.
16. Record the unit the contents were moved from in FL-EDMS.
17. Record the qualified backup unit the contents were moved to in FL-EDMS.
18. Record the transfer in accordance with SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
19. Notify Quality Assurance the same business day.
20. Assess the excursion under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

## 4. Control of this instruction

1. Quality Assurance and the Quality Control Supervisor approve this instruction and each revision of it.
2. Make each revision through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
