# PI-001 — Packaging and Labeling Instructions, FL-101 Drug Substance

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Manufacturing

## 1. Purpose

State the container, labeling, and overprinting requirements for filled containers of FL-101 drug substance, for use under SOP-QA-022, Labeling and Packaging Control, which governs the checking, issuance, and reconciliation of labeling.

## 2. Container and closure

1. Fill FL-101 drug substance into the container and closure specified in the batch's master batch record, issued under SOP-QA-027, Batch Record Content and Control.
2. Apply tamper-evident closure seals to every filled container.
3. Store filled containers at −20 °C ± 5 °C pending release under SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release.

## 3. Label content

Print or overprint each container label with the following, taken from the batch record for the batch:

| Field | Source |
|---|---|
| Product name: FL-101, monoclonal antibody drug substance | This instruction |
| Lot number | Batch record |
| Protein concentration (actual result) | Batch record, tested under TM-QC-101 |
| Container volume | Batch record |
| Storage statement: "Store at −20 °C ± 5 °C" | This instruction |
| Retest date | Batch record, as assigned under SOP-QC-002 |
| Statement: "For further manufacturing use only" | This instruction |
| Manufacturer: Fictitious Labs Inc. | This instruction |

## 4. Application

1. Perform line clearance before labeling begins, under SOP-QA-022, Labeling and Packaging Control.
2. Check the identity and content of labeling issued for the batch against this instruction and the batch production record before use, under SOP-QA-022.
3. Examine applied labels at 100 percent, by the control recorded for the line under SOP-QA-022.
4. Reconcile all printed labeling at 100 percent at the completion of labeling, under SOP-QA-022.
5. Obtain signed authorization for any deviation from this instruction before proceeding, under SOP-QA-022.

## 5. Control of this instruction

1. Quality Assurance and the Manufacturing Supervisor approve this instruction and each revision of it.
2. Make each revision through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
3. Include, in every revision, the label content fields of section 3 of this instruction; a field may be removed only with a documented justification approved by Quality Assurance.
