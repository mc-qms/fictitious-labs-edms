# SOP-QA-036 — Reprocessing, Reworking, and Returned Goods

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

This procedure states the restrictive position of Fictitious Labs Inc. on reprocessing, reworking, and returned goods. Reprocessing outside the approved master batch record, reworking, and release of returned product are not permitted except as expressly allowed under this procedure.

## 2. Scope

Applies to reprocessing, reworking, and returned goods at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Assurance, Manufacturing, Quality Control laboratory, Warehouse, Regulatory Affairs.

Does not cover recall returns, which are handled under SOP-QA-014, Product Recall, not this procedure. Does not cover routine in-process adjustments that are part of the established manufacturing process as written in the approved master batch record.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Quality Assurance**
- Owns this procedure.
- Approves or rejects any reprocessing not written into the approved master batch record before it is performed.
- Dispositions all returned product.
- Controls batch status under SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release.
- Directs destruction or return of rejected returned product.

**QA Manager**
- Approves or rejects a reworking proposal in advance.
- Approves the protocol under which any approved reworking is performed.
- Approves release of salvaged returned product.

**Regulatory Affairs**
- Assesses whether a proposed reworking operation affects a filing or established condition.
- Provides the assessment to Quality Assurance before a reworking approval is granted.

**Manufacturing Supervisor**
- Performs reprocessing only as written in the approved master batch record or as approved in advance by Quality Assurance.
- Submits reworking proposals to Quality Assurance for evaluation.
- Records each reprocessing step in the batch record.

**Warehouse Supervisor**
- Receives returned product under SOP-QA-023, Warehouse and Distribution Control.
- Segregates returned product from released material.
- Maintains returned product in segregation until Quality Assurance provides a disposition.

**Quality Control**
- Performs testing required by Quality Assurance on reprocessed material.
- Performs testing required by Quality Assurance on returned product under evaluation for salvage.
- Reports test results to Quality Assurance.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Reprocess a batch

1. Confirm the reprocessing step appears in FRM-MF-101, Master Batch Record, FL-101 Drug Substance, issued under SOP-QA-027, Batch Record Content and Control.
2. Perform the reprocessing step exactly as written when the step appears in FRM-MF-101, Master Batch Record, FL-101 Drug Substance.
3. Record the performance of the step in the batch record.
4. Stop the batch when a needed reprocessing step does not appear in FRM-MF-101, Master Batch Record, FL-101 Drug Substance.
5. Raise a deviation under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations for any reprocessing not written into the approved master batch record.
6. Submit the deviation to Quality Assurance for review.
7. Obtain prior written approval from Quality Assurance before performing the reprocessing.
8. Perform the approved reprocessing only after the written approval is received.
9. Submit samples of the reprocessed material to Quality Control for testing.
10. Confirm the reprocessed material meets SPEC-QC-101, FL-101 Drug Substance Specification.
11. Refer an out-of-specification result to SOP-QA-012, Out-of-Specification (OOS) Investigation.
12. Submit the completed batch record for review under SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release.
13. Release the batch only under SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release.
14. Refer any change to a reprocessing step written into the master batch record to SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
15. Refer the need for corrective or preventive action identified during reprocessing to SOP-QA-011, Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA).

### 4.2 Evaluate a reworking proposal

1. Submit a written reworking proposal to Quality Assurance.
2. Describe the nonconformity affecting the lot in the proposal.
3. Describe the proposed operation in the proposal.
4. Confirm the proposed operation is not part of the established manufacturing process.
5. Treat the lot as nonconforming until a disposition is issued.
6. Forward the proposal to Regulatory Affairs for assessment.
7. Assess whether the proposed operation affects a filing or established condition.
8. Provide the Regulatory Affairs assessment to Quality Assurance.
9. Prepare a protocol for the proposed reworking operation.
10. Submit the protocol to the QA Manager for approval.
11. Prohibit the reworking operation until the QA Manager approves the protocol in advance.
12. Perform approved reworking only in accordance with the approved protocol.
13. Submit samples of the reworked material to Quality Control for testing.
14. Confirm the reworked material meets SPEC-QC-101, FL-101 Drug Substance Specification.
15. Submit the completed batch record for review under SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release.
16. Retain a reserve sample of the reworked lot under SOP-QC-005, Retention (Reserve) Samples.
17. Refer the need for corrective or preventive action identified during evaluation to SOP-QA-011, Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA).

### 4.3 Receive and disposition returned goods

1. Receive returned product under SOP-QA-023, Warehouse and Distribution Control.
2. Place returned product in segregation on receipt.
3. Label the segregated product with its returned status.
4. Notify Quality Assurance of the receipt.
5. Record the receipt in FL-EDMS.
6. Examine the returned product for evidence of damage.
7. Examine the returned product for evidence of improper storage.
8. Examine the returned product for evidence of tampering.
9. Review the available distribution records for the returned product.
10. Evaluate whether the identity of the product can be assured.
11. Evaluate whether the strength of the product can be assured.
12. Evaluate whether the quality of the product can be assured.
13. Evaluate whether the purity of the product can be assured.
14. Reject product whose identity cannot be assured.
15. Reject product whose strength cannot be assured.
16. Reject product whose quality cannot be assured.
17. Reject product whose purity cannot be assured.
18. Destroy rejected product as Quality Assurance directs.
19. Return rejected product as Quality Assurance directs when destruction is not directed.
20. Record the disposition in FL-EDMS.
21. Change the batch status only as directed by Quality Assurance under SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release.
22. Refer a returned product that is the subject of a recall to SOP-QA-014, Product Recall.

### 4.4 Salvage returned product

1. Identify returned product under consideration for salvage.
2. Submit the returned product to Quality Assurance for evaluation.
3. Complete the evaluation Quality Assurance requires.
4. Submit samples to Quality Control for the testing Quality Assurance requires.
5. Review the test results against SPEC-QC-101, FL-101 Drug Substance Specification.
6. Refer an out-of-specification result to SOP-QA-012, Out-of-Specification (OOS) Investigation.
7. Record the evaluation in FL-EDMS.
8. Record any testing performed in FL-EDMS.
9. Obtain approval from the QA Manager before release of the salvaged product.
10. Release salvaged product only after the required evaluation is complete.
11. Release salvaged product only after the required testing is complete.
12. Retain a reserve sample of the salvaged lot under SOP-QC-005, Retention (Reserve) Samples.
13. Reject returned product that does not meet the requirements for salvage.
14. Destroy rejected product as Quality Assurance directs.
15. Record the final disposition in FL-EDMS.

## 5. Records

Records generated under this procedure include deviations, reworking proposals, reworking protocols, Regulatory Affairs assessments, returned product receipts, disposition records, salvage evaluations, and test results. Each record identifies the lot, the material, the decision made, and the person who made the decision. Records are recorded in FL-EDMS or in the batch record, as applicable. Completed records are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
