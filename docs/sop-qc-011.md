# SOP-QC-011 — Microbiological Media Preparation, Growth Promotion Testing, and Laboratory Autoclaves

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Control

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how microbiological media are prepared, growth promotion tested, and released for laboratory use. It also describes the operation of laboratory autoclaves used to sterilize media and laboratory items.

## 2. Scope

Applies to microbiological media preparation, growth promotion testing, and laboratory autoclaves at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Control laboratory, Quality Assurance.

Does not cover the requirements that invoke growth promotion testing in other procedures. Growth promotion requirements are invoked by SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills, and SOP-QC-001, Environmental Monitoring; this procedure governs the laboratory operation itself. Biohazard waste autoclaving is governed by SOP-QA-031, Biosafety, Biohazard Waste, and Spill Response.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Analyst (Microbiology)**
- Prepare media as described in this procedure.
- Perform growth promotion testing as described in this procedure.
- Perform sterility checks of uninoculated units as described in this procedure.
- Operate laboratory autoclaves as described in this procedure.
- Record each preparation and test in the media record.
- Report a growth promotion or biological-indicator failure to the Microbiology Supervisor.

**Microbiology Supervisor**
- Review media preparation records.
- Review growth promotion test records.
- Review sterility check records.
- Review laboratory autoclave records.
- Confirm that a batch or load that failed is not used.
- Assess the effect of a failure on results already generated.

**Quality Assurance**
- Approve reliance on a supplier's certification for purchased ready-to-use media under SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality.
- Review assessments of results generated with affected media.
- Oversee handling of a failure under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
- Oversee investigation of a reported result in question under SOP-QA-012, Out-of-Specification (OOS) Investigation.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Prepare media

1. Confirm the media to be prepared from the laboratory schedule.
2. Gather the materials listed for the preparation.
3. Record the materials used in the media record.
4. Prepare the media as stated in the media record.
5. Assign a batch identifier to the prepared media.
6. Record the batch identifier in the media record.
7. Label each container with the batch identifier.
8. Sterilize the media as described in Section 4.3, where sterilization is required.
9. Set aside units for growth promotion testing.
10. Set aside uninoculated units for the sterility check.
11. Hold the batch until growth promotion testing and the sterility check are complete.
12. Store the media as stated in the media record.
13. Use the media only within the expiry or holding period stated in the media record.
14. Record the storage location in the media record.
15. Do not use media past the expiry or holding period stated in the media record.

### 4.2 Perform growth promotion testing

1. Confirm that every batch of media prepared in the laboratory receives growth promotion testing before the batch is used.
2. Test the batch with the designated organism panel.
3. Use the designated organism panel as the designated group of reference microorganisms including representative local isolates, per SOP-QC-001, Environmental Monitoring.
4. Perform a sterility check of uninoculated units for the batch.
5. Record the growth promotion results in the media record.
6. Record the sterility check results in the media record.
7. Compare the results against the acceptance stated in the media record.
8. Mark the batch as accepted in the media record when the results meet the acceptance stated in the media record.
9. Release an accepted batch for laboratory use.
10. Test each lot of purchased ready-to-use media for growth promotion on receipt.
11. Check whether Quality Assurance has approved reliance on the supplier's certification under SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality, for a lot of purchased ready-to-use media.
12. Omit receipt growth promotion testing only for a lot covered by that approved reliance.
13. Record the basis for receipt testing or approved reliance in the media record.
14. Note that APS media acceptance (growth promotion with the designated panel) is required by SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills.
15. Hold media from use until its growth promotion testing is complete.
16. Report any growth promotion failure as described in Section 4.4.

### 4.3 Operate laboratory autoclaves

1. Confirm that the laboratory autoclave is available for use.
2. Load the media or laboratory items into the autoclave.
3. Record the contents of the load in the autoclave record.
4. Place a chemical indicator in each load.
5. Start the sterilization cycle for the load.
6. Monitor the cycle until it completes.
7. Remove the load after the cycle completes.
8. Check the chemical indicator for the load.
9. Record the chemical indicator result in the autoclave record.
10. Record the completed cycle in the autoclave record.
11. Perform biological-indicator testing of the cycle weekly.
12. Record the biological-indicator result in the autoclave record.
13. Note that weekly biological-indicator testing is the same convention as the biohazard-waste kill cycle in SOP-QA-031, Biosafety, Biohazard Waste, and Spill Response.
14. Refer autoclave maintenance needs under SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance.
15. Hold the sterilized media or items from use until the load record is complete.
16. Report any biological-indicator failure as described in Section 4.4.

### 4.4 Respond to a failure

1. Stop use of the media batch or autoclave load when a growth promotion or biological-indicator failure occurs.
2. Reject the media batch or load.
3. Label the rejected batch or load as not for use.
4. Segregate the rejected batch or load from accepted media and items.
5. Notify the Microbiology Supervisor of the failure.
6. Notify Quality Assurance of the failure.
7. Record the failure in the media record or autoclave record, as applicable.
8. Identify results generated with the affected media.
9. Assess results generated with affected media under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
10. Assess a reported result in question under SOP-QA-012, Out-of-Specification (OOS) Investigation, where a reported result is in question.
11. Record the assessment in FL-EDMS.
12. Do not use the rejected batch or load.
13. Close the failure record only after the assessment is complete.

## 5. Records

Media preparation records document each batch of media prepared in the laboratory. Growth promotion test records document the designated organism panel used, the results for each batch or lot, and the sterility check of uninoculated units. Laboratory autoclave records document each load, its chemical indicator result, and the weekly biological-indicator testing of the cycle.

Media preparation and test records are held in FL-EDMS (the company EDMS). Autoclave records are also held in FL-EDMS. Records are completed and kept in accordance with SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices, and are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
