# SOP-QA-028 — Specification Management

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

Establish how specifications are proposed, justified, approved, applied, and revised, so that every acceptance criterion applied to a product, material, or in-process result has a documented basis and an accountable approval.

## 2. Scope

Applies to every specification at Fictitious Labs Inc., including the FL-101 drug substance specification, component and material specifications, and in-process specifications, in the following functions: Quality Assurance, Quality Control laboratory, Manufacturing.

Does not cover the validation of the analytical procedures that execute specification tests, which follows SOP-QC-003, Analytical Procedure Validation. Does not cover the investigation of a result that fails a specification, which follows SOP-QA-012, Out-of-Specification (OOS) Investigation.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Quality Control**
  - Draft each specification and the rationale for its acceptance criteria.
  - Maintain the specification register in FL-EDMS.

- **Process Owner**
  - Provide the process capability and stability data on which proposed criteria rest.
  - Confirm that each proposed criterion is achievable by the process as validated.

- **Quality Assurance**
  - Quality Assurance is the Quality Unit function designated for this procedure: Quality Assurance reviews and approves every specification before use.
  - Confirm that each acceptance criterion has a documented justification before approval.

- **QA Manager**
  - Approve the initial issue of a product specification.
  - Approve any revision that widens an acceptance criterion.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Establish a specification

1. Identify each test the specification must contain, from the quality attributes of the product or material and the expectations of the governing regulations.
2. Reference the analytical procedure that performs each test, including:
   - The test method number, where a numbered test method exists.
   - The validated procedure approved under SOP-QC-003, Analytical Procedure Validation, where it does not.
3. Propose an acceptance criterion for each test.
4. Justify each proposed criterion in a rationale record, using the applicable data, including:
   - Process capability data from validation and routine manufacture.
   - Stability data generated under SOP-QC-002, Stability Testing.
   - The capability and variability of the analytical procedure.
   - Any limit set by regulation or compendial requirement.
5. State the stage at which each test applies, as release, stability, in-process, or receipt.
6. Submit the draft specification and its rationale record to Quality Assurance for approval.

### 4.2 Approve a specification

1. Review the draft specification against its rationale record.
2. Confirm that every acceptance criterion is supported by the data cited for it.
3. Obtain QA Manager approval for the initial issue of a product specification.
4. Approve the specification in FL-EDMS.
5. Make the approved specification effective in FL-EDMS under SOP-QA-003, Document Control.
6. Withdraw superseded versions of the specification from use under SOP-QA-003, Document Control.

### 4.3 Apply a specification

1. Cite the specification, by number and version, in each test method and record that applies it.
2. Compare each result with the version of the specification effective on the date the material was manufactured or the test was performed.
3. Report each result under SOP-QA-002, Reporting Numerical Values.
4. Refer any result that fails the specification for investigation under SOP-QA-012, Out-of-Specification (OOS) Investigation.

### 4.4 Revise a specification

1. Initiate every revision through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
2. Support the proposed revision with data in an updated rationale record.
3. Obtain QA Manager approval for any revision that widens an acceptance criterion.
4. Never revise a specification in response to a failing batch or a failing result.
5. Assess the effect of the approved revision on batches already manufactured, and document the assessment in the change record.

### 4.5 Review specifications

1. Review each product specification as part of the Annual Product Quality Review under SOP-QA-021, Annual Product Quality Review.
2. Revise the specification under section 4.4 when the review shows a criterion is no longer supported by process or stability data.

## 5. Records

Specifications, current and superseded; rationale records for initial criteria and for each revision; and the specification register in FL-EDMS, retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
