# WI-MF-004 — Airlock Pass-Through and Material Transfer

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Manufacturing

## 1. Purpose

This instruction describes how to prepare an item and pass it through an airlock into a controlled area. It sits under SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills, and the contamination control strategy of SOP-QA-029, Contamination Control Strategy, is the governing policy for it.

## 2. Prepare an item for transfer

1. Confirm that personnel handling the item are gowned under SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills.
2. Check whether the transfer is into Grade A or Grade B.
3. Check whether SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills, requires Quality Assurance approval of an exception for the transfer.
4. Obtain Quality Assurance approval of the exception in advance where SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills, requires it.
5. Record the approved transfer exception in FL-EDMS.
6. Do not start the transfer until an exception required by SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills, has been approved.
7. Check the item for cardboard packaging before it enters a Grade B corridor.
8. Check the item for cardboard packaging before it enters a Grade C corridor.
9. Remove cardboard from the item before it enters a Grade B corridor.
10. Remove cardboard from the item before it enters a Grade C corridor.
11. Remove the outer packaging from the item before the airlock.
12. Place the item where it can be sprayed and wiped.
13. Select a disinfectant from the cleaning program in SOP-QA-034, Routine Facility Cleaning, Sanitization, and Sporicidal Rotation.
14. Spray the item with the selected disinfectant.
15. Wipe the item with the selected disinfectant.
16. Hold the item for the wet contact time given in the program in SOP-QA-034, Routine Facility Cleaning, Sanitization, and Sporicidal Rotation.
17. Do not move the item into the airlock before the wet contact time has passed.
18. Check that the outer packaging has been removed from the item.
19. Check that cardboard has been removed from the item.
20. Check that the item has been sprayed with the selected disinfectant.
21. Check that the item has been wiped with the selected disinfectant.
22. Check that the item has been held for the wet contact time.
23. Confirm that personnel handling the item remain gowned under SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills.
24. Confirm that the transfer follows SOP-QA-029, Contamination Control Strategy.
25. Record the transfer in accordance with SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.

## 3. Pass the item through the airlock

1. Place the prepared item in the airlock.
2. Close the airlock door behind the item.
3. Confirm that only one airlock door is open at any time.
4. Do not open both interlocked airlock doors together.
5. Do not prop open an interlocked airlock door.
6. Report a propped-open interlock to the Manufacturing Supervisor on the same day it is found.
7. Remove the item from the airlock on the controlled-area side.
8. Close the airlock door after the item is removed.
9. Check that no cardboard has entered a Grade B corridor with the item.
10. Check that no cardboard has entered a Grade C corridor with the item.
11. Check that no outer packaging removed before the airlock has entered with the item.
12. Move the item on from the airlock after it is removed.
13. Report a failure of the airlock interlock to the Manufacturing Supervisor on the same day it is found.
14. Raise a deviation for a transfer that does not follow this instruction under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
15. Check that both interlocked airlock doors are closed after the transfer.
16. Check that no interlocked airlock door has been left propped open after the transfer.
17. Check that the approved transfer exception, where one was required, is recorded in FL-EDMS.
18. Record the completed transfer in accordance with SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.

## 4. Control of this instruction

1. Quality Assurance and the Manufacturing Supervisor approve this instruction and each revision of it.
2. Make each revision through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
