# SOP-QA-005 — Training and Qualification

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

Establish how personnel are trained and qualified for the tasks they perform, and how training is recorded and kept current.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the training and qualification of all personnel whose work affects product quality at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Assurance, Quality Control laboratory, Manufacturing.

Does not cover professional licensing or continuing education outside the quality system.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Trainee**
  - Complete assigned training and qualification before performing the task.
  - Do not perform a task affecting product quality until trained and qualified for that task. The training record, not the assignment, is evidence of training.
  - Document completed training and qualification in the training record in FL-EDMS, by attestation recorded with the completion.

- **Trainer**
  - Provide assigned training.
  - Assess whether the trainee can perform the task as required.
  - Be qualified on the task being taught and designated as a trainer by the Supervisor.
  - Assess qualification by documented observed performance against the procedure — and, for laboratory methods, by acceptable qualification results where the method requires them.
  - Record the assessment in the training record.

- **Supervisor**
  - Ensure personnel are trained and qualified before assigning them tasks that affect product quality.
  - Identify training needed for assigned duties.
  - Arrange initial and continuing training. Training should be appropriate to the duties assigned, and its practical effectiveness should be assessed periodically.
  - When a document revision affects a task, assess who must be retrained and on what changed. Make the assessment proportionate to the change.
  - Manage training assignments for their personnel from the training matrix in FL-EDMS. Document Control reports overdue training monthly, and Quality Assurance reviews overdue GMP training monthly with the Supervisor.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Identify training needs

1. Identify the training needed for each task affecting product quality, including training appropriate to the duties assigned.
2. Provide training on the quality system and GMP, as applicable to the person's duties.
3. Task-specific training requirements are established in the training matrix in FL-EDMS, which states for each role the procedures and tasks that role performs. The matrix is approved by the function Supervisor and Quality Assurance before personnel are trained against it, and assignments are made from the matrix.

### 4.2 Train and qualify personnel

1. Complete required training and qualification before performing an assigned task affecting product quality. Do not treat assignment to a task as evidence of training or qualification.
2. For personnel working where contamination is a hazard, provide specific training relevant to that hazard.
3. Assess the practical effectiveness of training periodically. The Supervisor assesses each person's practical effectiveness for their assigned tasks at least annually, based on observed work and record quality, and documents the assessment in the training record in FL-EDMS.
4. Qualification follows the assigned training: the trainee performs the task under observation.
5. The Trainer assesses the performance against the procedure.
6. Qualification is demonstrated by the training record in FL-EDMS carrying the Trainer's assessment and the Supervisor's confirmation, each by electronic signature. Until qualification is documented in FL-EDMS, the person may perform the task only under the direct supervision of a person qualified for it.

### 4.3 Keep training current

1. Provide continuing training appropriate to personnel's duties and needs.
2. When a document revision affects a task, assess who must be retrained and what changed. Set the retraining response in proportion to the change. The Supervisor determines who must be retrained: where the revision changes how a task is performed, retraining is completed before the revision's effective date, as required by SOP-QA-003, Document Control; where the revision is editorial, read-and-understand notification is sufficient.
3. Document Control monitors training currency from FL-EDMS.
4. Document Control reports overdue training monthly to Supervisors and Quality Assurance.
5. Retraining decisions and completions are documented in the training records in FL-EDMS.

### 4.4 Address training gaps

1. The Supervisor owns each training gap for their personnel. While a gap remains open, the person does not perform the affected task unsupervised.
2. The Supervisor documents the gap, the restriction, and the plan and date for completing the training in FL-EDMS.
3. If work was performed by a person who was not qualified for the task, the Supervisor escalates it to Quality Assurance the same business day.
4. The work is assessed as a deviation under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

## 5. Records

Training and qualification records for each person, in FL-EDMS — training matrix assignments, completions and attestations, qualification assessments and sign-offs, retraining decisions, and periodic effectiveness assessments — retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
