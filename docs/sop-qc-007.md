# SOP-QC-007 — Analytical Method Transfer and Compendial Verification

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Control

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how analytical procedures are transferred between laboratories and how compendial procedures are verified before use for GMP testing.
It provides a protocol-based approach with acceptance criteria set in advance, so that a receiving laboratory demonstrates it can perform a procedure reliably under its own conditions of use.

## 2. Scope

Applies to analytical method transfers and compendial verifications at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Control laboratory, Quality Assurance. Transfers run between the Fictitious Labs QC laboratory and contract laboratories, or between internal laboratory areas. Compendial verifications apply to compendial procedures used by the Quality Control laboratory for GMP testing.

Does not cover analytical procedure validation, which is covered by SOP-QC-003, Analytical Procedure Validation; routine stability testing, which is covered by SOP-QC-002, Stability Testing; or the qualification of contract laboratories, which is covered by SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality. This procedure closes the exclusion declared in SOP-QC-003, Analytical Procedure Validation, which does not cover method transfer or compendial verification.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Study Director**

- Owns the method transfer or compendial verification.
- Prepares the transfer or verification protocol.
- Sets the acceptance criteria before any transfer or verification testing begins.
- Prepares the transfer or verification report.

**Analyst**

- Performs transfer or verification testing as written in the approved protocol.
- Records test data in accordance with SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
- Reports any discrepancy or unexpected result to the Laboratory Supervisor promptly.

**Laboratory Supervisor**

- Assigns analysts to perform the protocol testing.
- Confirms that laboratory equipment required by the protocol is available for use.
- Confirms that assigned analysts have completed the training required for the procedure.
- Reviews laboratory test data for completeness before submission to the Study Director.

**Quality Assurance**

- Approves the protocol before testing begins.
- Approves the report before the receiving laboratory uses the procedure for GMP testing.
- Records each approval in FL-EDMS.
- Provides disposition for discrepancies and failures arising during transfer or verification testing.

**QA Manager**

- Reviews escalated discrepancies that affect the conclusion of a transfer or verification.
- Provides final Quality Assurance disposition when protocol acceptance criteria are not met.

## 4. Procedure

The transfer and verification design in this procedure rests on protocol-defined comparative testing and predefined acceptance criteria.
No public equivalent of USP <1224> or USP <1226> detail is relied upon, and no compendial chapter text is reproduced in protocols written under this procedure.
Compendial procedures are cited by chapter number and title only, for example USP <85> Bacterial Endotoxins Test, USP <785> Osmolality and Osmolarity, and USP <788> Particulate Matter in Injections.

### 4.1 Plan the transfer or verification

1. Identify the analytical procedure to be transferred or verified.
2. Determine whether the activity is a method transfer or a compendial verification.
3. Identify the sending laboratory for a method transfer.
4. Identify the receiving laboratory for a method transfer.
5. Confirm that a contract laboratory taking part in a transfer is classified as a Critical supplier under SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality.
6. Define the samples to be tested under the protocol.
7. Define the sample matrices to be tested under the protocol.
8. Define the equipment to be used under the protocol.
9. Define the analysts who will perform the testing under the protocol.
10. Select the reference standards required by the protocol in accordance with SOP-QC-006, Reference Standards and Laboratory Controls.
11. Confirm the current specification for the material under SOP-QA-028, Specification Management.
12. Set the acceptance criteria for the transfer or verification.
13. Record the acceptance criteria in the protocol before any testing begins.
14. Prepare the transfer or verification protocol.
15. Submit the protocol to Quality Assurance for approval.
16. Record the Quality Assurance approval of the protocol in FL-EDMS.
17. Confirm that the protocol approval is recorded before testing begins.

For a compendial verification, the protocol states the actual conditions of use at Fictitious Labs.
Those conditions cover the laboratory's samples, matrices, equipment, and analysts.
Verification does not repeat validation.
Verification does not apply to procedures validated under SOP-QC-003, Analytical Procedure Validation.

### 4.2 Execute the protocol

1. Assign the analysts named in the protocol to the testing.
2. Prepare the samples specified in the protocol.
3. Prepare the reference standards specified in the protocol in accordance with SOP-QC-006, Reference Standards and Laboratory Controls.
4. Perform the sending laboratory testing specified in the protocol for a method transfer.
5. Perform the receiving laboratory testing specified in the protocol for a method transfer.
6. Perform the verification testing specified in the protocol under the laboratory's actual conditions of use for a compendial verification.
7. Record each test result at the time the test is performed.
8. Record test data in accordance with SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
9. Identify each sample container with its protocol identifier.
10. Report any discrepancy observed during testing to the Laboratory Supervisor.
11. Refer a discrepancy or failure against protocol criteria for handling under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
12. Refer an out-of-specification result obtained during transfer or verification testing for handling under SOP-QA-012, Out-of-Specification (OOS) Investigation.
13. Preserve all raw data generated under the protocol for inclusion in the report file.
14. Notify the Study Director when protocol testing is complete.

### 4.3 Evaluate results against the criteria

1. Compile the test results generated under the protocol.
2. Compare the receiving laboratory results with the sending laboratory results for a method transfer.
3. Compare the verification results with the conditions of use stated in the protocol for a compendial verification.
4. Compare each result with the acceptance criteria recorded in the protocol.
5. Identify each result that does not meet the acceptance criteria.
6. Refer each discrepancy or failure against protocol criteria for handling under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
7. Refer each out-of-specification result for handling under SOP-QA-012, Out-of-Specification (OOS) Investigation.
8. Determine whether corrective and preventive action is required under SOP-QA-011, Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA).
9. Summarize the test results in the transfer or verification report.
10. State the conclusion of the transfer or verification in the report.
11. State each unresolved discrepancy in the report.
12. Prepare the transfer or verification report.
13. Submit the report to Quality Assurance for approval.

### 4.4 Approve the procedure for use at the receiving laboratory

1. Review the transfer or verification report against the approved protocol.
2. Approve the report when the acceptance criteria recorded in the protocol are met.
3. Record the Quality Assurance approval of the report in FL-EDMS.
4. Confirm that the report approval is recorded in FL-EDMS.
5. Notify the receiving laboratory of the report approval.
6. Authorize the receiving laboratory to use the procedure for GMP testing only after the report approval is recorded.
7. File the approved protocol in FL-EDMS.
8. File the approved report in FL-EDMS.
9. Refer any proposed change to the transferred or verified procedure for handling under SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
10. Refer any proposed change to a related specification for handling under SOP-QA-028, Specification Management.

## 5. Records

Records generated under this procedure include approved transfer and verification protocols, raw test data, deviation and out-of-specification records referenced by the protocol, and approved transfer and verification reports. Protocol and report approvals are recorded in FL-EDMS. Records are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
