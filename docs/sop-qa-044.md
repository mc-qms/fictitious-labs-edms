# SOP-QA-044 — GMP Spare Parts Control and Post-Maintenance Return-to-Service

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how GMP spare parts are controlled at Fictitious Labs Inc.
It also describes how equipment is returned to service after maintenance.

## 2. Scope

Applies to GMP spare parts control and the return of equipment to service after maintenance at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Engineering and Maintenance, Quality Assurance, Manufacturing, Quality Control laboratory.

Does not cover calibration and preventive maintenance schedules or the equipment register itself.
Calibration and preventive maintenance schedules and the equipment register itself are governed by SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance.
This procedure covers spare parts control and the return of equipment to service after maintenance.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Maintenance Technician**

- Raises corrective repairs as work orders in FL-EDMS.
- Replaces spare parts in accordance with Section 4.2.
- Records parts used in the work order.
- Completes the work order in FL-EDMS after maintenance.
- Tags defective equipment out of service.

**Equipment Owner**

- Verifies maintenance work after completion.
- Authorizes return to use after repair, with Quality Assurance concurrence for GMP-critical equipment.
- Confirms cleaning is completed before return to service where it is required.

**Quality Assurance**

- Provides concurrence for the return to service of GMP-critical equipment.
- Handles equipment returned to service without the required verification or concurrence under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

**Manufacturing Supervisor**

- Confirms equipment status before manufacturing use.
- Removes out-of-service equipment from manufacturing use when it is tagged out of service.
- Checks equipment status tagging before manufacturing use.
- Stops manufacturing use when equipment is tagged out of service.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Control spare parts

1. Identify GMP-critical spare parts in the equipment register in FL-EDMS under SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance.
2. Store product-contact spare parts segregated from other parts.
3. Keep product-contact spare parts clean during storage.
4. Label product-contact spare parts with the equipment they serve.
5. Check the label on a spare part before removing the part from storage.
6. Return an unused spare part to its labeled storage location.
7. Remove a damaged spare part from GMP storage.
8. Tag defective equipment out of service in accordance with WI-MF-003, Clean/Dirty Hold Time and Equipment Status Tagging.
9. Tag defective equipment out of service in accordance with SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance.
10. Keep segregated storage in place for product-contact spare parts.
11. Keep the equipment label with the stored product-contact spare part.
12. Check stored product-contact spare parts for cleanliness before use.

### 4.2 Replace a part

1. Raise a work order in FL-EDMS for a corrective repair in accordance with SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance.
2. Select a spare part labeled for the equipment to be repaired.
3. Compare the replacement part with the part to be replaced.
4. Replace the part with an identical part as a like-for-like replacement.
5. Stop the replacement when the replacement part is not identical to the part it replaces.
6. Process a part that is not identical to the part it replaces as a change through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
7. Install the replacement part.
8. Record each part used in the work order.
9. Record the equipment served in the work order.
10. Keep the removed part with the work order record until the work order is completed.
11. Confirm the installed part matches the part recorded in the work order.

### 4.3 Return equipment to service after maintenance

1. Complete the required maintenance under the work order.
2. Complete cleaning under SOP-QA-034, Routine Facility Cleaning, Sanitization, and Sporicidal Rotation where maintenance touches a classified area.
3. Complete cleaning under SOP-QA-034, Routine Facility Cleaning, Sanitization, and Sporicidal Rotation where maintenance touches a product-contact surface.
4. Complete the work order in FL-EDMS as the Maintenance Technician.
5. Verify the completed work as the Equipment Owner.
6. Obtain Quality Assurance concurrence before returning GMP-critical equipment to service.
7. Authorize return to use after repair as the Equipment Owner.
8. Provide Quality Assurance concurrence for GMP-critical equipment before its return to service.
9. Update the equipment status after authorization.
10. Confirm the equipment status before manufacturing use as the Manufacturing Supervisor.
11. Handle equipment returned to service without the required verification, discovered after the fact, under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
12. Handle equipment returned to service without the required concurrence, discovered after the fact, under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

## 5. Records

Work orders are completed in FL-EDMS and record the maintenance performed and the parts used.
Work order records also record Equipment Owner verification of the work.
Work order records for GMP-critical equipment also record Quality Assurance concurrence for return to service.
Records of cleaning completed before return to service are recorded where cleaning under SOP-QA-034, Routine Facility Cleaning, Sanitization, and Sporicidal Rotation is required.
All records generated under this procedure are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
