# SOP-QA-008 — Management Review

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

Establish how senior management reviews the performance of the quality system at planned intervals and directs its improvement.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the periodic review of quality-system performance across all functions at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States. Quality Assurance coordinates the review; senior management chairs it.

Does not cover product-specific quality reviews, which follow SOP-QA-021, Annual Product Quality Review.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Senior Management**
  - Chair the review.
  - Review the quality system periodically for continuing suitability and effectiveness.
  - Assess review conclusions.
  - Direct appropriate improvements, resource allocation or reallocation, personnel training, and revisions to quality policy or objectives.

- **QA Manager (Management Representative)**
  - Act as management representative for the review: convene it, prepare the input package, record minutes and actions, track actions to closure, and escalate issues requiring senior management decision.

- **Quality Assurance**
  - Prepare, coordinate, document, and follow up the management review: compile the inputs from FL-EDMS, maintain the review's indicator set and template, and monitor action completion.

- **Process Owners**
  - Provide the review inputs for their areas.
  - Present them as required.
  - Carry out the actions assigned to them by their due dates.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Plan the review

1. Review the quality system at least annually.
2. The QA Manager schedules the review in FL-EDMS.
3. The QA Manager convenes it.
4. Senior management chairs it. Participants are the QA Manager and the heads of Quality Control, Manufacturing, Regulatory Affairs, and Document Control; other personnel attend as the inputs require.
5. Quality Assurance prepares the input package from FL-EDMS data.
6. Quality Assurance distributes it to participants at least 5 business days before the review.

### 4.2 Compile review inputs

1. The review period is the preceding 12 months, or the period since the last review if shorter. Inputs are collected from the systems of record in FL-EDMS and verified by the process owners for their areas before the review.
2. Assess progress toward quality-system objectives.
3. Review the performance indicators used to monitor the effectiveness of quality-system processes, as defined in the management review template maintained by Quality Assurance in FL-EDMS. Consider indicators concerning:
   - Complaints, deviations, corrective and preventive actions (CAPA), and change management.
   - Feedback on outsourced activities and supplier performance.
   - Self-assessments, including risk assessments, trending, and internal audits under SOP-QA-007.
   - External assessments, including regulatory inspections and findings, and customer audits.
   - The annual product quality review under SOP-QA-021, out-of-specification and complaint trends, training status, and overdue items of every kind.
4. Consider internal and external factors that may affect the quality system. These may include emerging regulations, guidance and quality issues; relevant innovations; changes in the business environment or objectives; and changes in product ownership.

### 4.3 Evaluate the quality system

1. Assess the review inputs and determine whether the quality system remains suitable and effective.
2. Evaluate performance against the quality objectives and the indicator set.
3. Identify issues for escalation — including overdue actions, adverse trends, and repeat findings.
4. Assess proposed improvements or actions for benefit, risk, and resource need.

### 4.4 Direct and follow up on outcomes

1. Determine and document review outcomes. Outcomes may include improvements to the quality system or related processes; allocation or reallocation of resources or personnel training; and revisions to quality policy or objectives.
2. Record each action in FL-EDMS with an owner and a due date.
3. Quality Assurance tracks actions; a systemic fix is effectiveness-checked, through SOP-QA-011, Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA), where a CAPA is opened for it.
4. The QA Manager closes actions when the completion evidence is recorded.
5. Quality Assurance issues the minutes to participants and function heads within 15 business days of the review.
6. Issues requiring senior management decision outside the review are escalated by the QA Manager, or by the chair, as they arise.

## 5. Records

Management review records, including the input package, minutes, decisions, and action records with their closure evidence, retained in FL-EDMS under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
