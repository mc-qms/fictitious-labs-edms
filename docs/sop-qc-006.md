# SOP-QC-006 — Reference Standards and Laboratory Controls

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Control

## 1. Purpose

Govern the reference standards the Quality Control laboratory measures against, and the laboratory's general controls over samples, reagents, and records, so that every reported result traces to a qualified standard and a complete record.

## 2. Scope

Applies to every reference standard, sample, reagent, and laboratory record in the Quality Control laboratory.

Does not cover the validation of analytical procedures, which follows SOP-QC-003, Analytical Procedure Validation; specifications, which follow SOP-QA-028, Specification Management; or the calibration and preventive maintenance of laboratory instruments, which follow SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Quality Control**
  - Qualify, store, and use reference standards under this procedure.
  - Receive and handle laboratory samples.
  - Prepare and label reagents and solutions.
  - Keep complete laboratory records for every test performed.

- **Quality Assurance**
  - Quality Assurance is the Quality Unit function designated for this procedure: Quality Assurance reviews and approves the qualification of each in-house reference standard before the standard is used.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Establish a reference standard

1. Use a compendial reference standard as the primary standard where one exists for the test.
2. Qualify an in-house primary standard against the compendial standard or, where no compendial standard exists, by characterization.
3. Qualify each working standard against the primary standard before use.
4. Assign each standard:
   - A unique identifier.
   - Its storage conditions.
   - An expiry or retest date.
5. Submit the qualification report to Quality Assurance for approval.
6. Record each approved standard in the reference standard register in FL-EDMS.

### 4.2 Store and use reference standards

1. Store each standard under its assigned conditions.
2. Cite the standard's identifier in each test record that uses it.
3. Remove each standard from use on its expiry or retest date.
4. Requalify a standard, with Quality Assurance approval, before any use after that date.
5. Report any use of an expired standard to Quality Assurance as a deviation under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

### 4.3 Receive and handle samples

1. Log each sample on receipt, recording:
   - The sample identity.
   - The lot number.
   - The quantity received.
   - The condition on receipt.
   - The date of receipt.
   - The receiving analyst.
2. Store each sample under the conditions its test method requires.
3. Label each aliquot taken from a sample with the sample identity and lot number.
4. Record each transfer of a sample between analysts in the laboratory record.

### 4.4 Prepare and label reagents

1. Prepare each reagent or solution according to the test method or a documented preparation instruction.
2. Label each container with:
   - The reagent name.
   - The concentration.
   - The preparation date.
   - The expiry date.
   - The preparer's initials.
3. Do not use a reagent or solution after its expiry date.

### 4.5 Keep laboratory records

1. Record for each test:
   - The test method number and version used.
   - The analyst who performed the test.
   - The date of the test.
   - The raw data.
   - The calculations.
   - The result.
2. Obtain a second-person verification of calculations and data transcription where the test method requires one.
3. Report each result under SOP-QA-002, Reporting Numerical Values.
4. Correct a laboratory record only under SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.

## 5. Records

The reference standard register in FL-EDMS and standard qualification reports; sample receipt logs; reagent preparation records; laboratory test records, retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
