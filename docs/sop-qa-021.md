# SOP-QA-021 — Annual Product Quality Review

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

Establish how the quality of each product is reviewed at least annually, so that trends, recurring problems, and the need for change are identified from the year's data.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the periodic quality review of every marketed product of Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Assurance, Quality Control laboratory.

Does not cover management review of the quality system as a whole, which follows SOP-QA-008, Management Review.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Report Author**
  - A Quality Assurance reviewer designated by the QA Manager for each review.
  - Compile the review from the records for the review period.
  - Document the trend assessments and conclusions.
  - Route the report for approval.

- **Process Owners**
  - Provide and verify the information for their areas.
  - Assess it with the Report Author.
  - Own the actions assigned to them.

- **Quality Assurance**
  - Review the report and its supporting information.
  - Decide the resulting actions with the process owners.
  - Track actions to completion.
  - The QA Manager approves each report.

- **Senior Management**
  - Assess the conclusions of periodic reviews of process performance and product quality.
  - Receive timely communication and escalation of appropriate quality issues.
  - Identify appropriate actions through management review, including improvements to manufacturing processes and products; provision, training, or realignment of resources; and capture and dissemination of knowledge.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Plan the review

1. Conduct a periodic quality review for each marketed product at least annually. Take previous reviews into account.
2. Document the periodic quality review for each marketed product.
3. The review period is the calendar year.
4. The Report Author prepares the report (number format APQR-<year> for the product, e.g., APQR-2026 for FL-101).
5. Quality Assurance reviews the report.
6. The QA Manager approves the report. Each report is completed by March 31 of the following year.
7. Quality Assurance tracks completion in FL-EDMS.
8. A review may cover more than one product only when the grouping is scientifically justified.
9. Quality Assurance determines whether products may be grouped.
10. The justification and the coverage of each product are documented in the report.

### 4.2 Compile and review product information

For the review period defined in section 4.1, the Report Author compiles the following from the records in FL-EDMS. The process owner for each category verifies the information for their area:

- Starting materials and packaging materials used, with particular attention to materials from new sources and traceability of active substances through the supply chain.
- Critical in-process controls and finished product results.
- All batches that failed to meet an established specification, including their investigations.
- Significant deviations and non-conformances, their investigations, and the effectiveness of related corrective and preventive actions.
- Changes to processes or analytical methods.
- Post-approval changes submitted to regulators, approved, or refused, including changes for export-only dossiers.
- Stability monitoring results and any adverse trends.
- Quality-related returns, complaints, and recalls, including investigations performed.
- The adequacy of previous corrective actions relating to the product, process, or equipment.
- Post-marketing commitments for new marketing authorizations and for approved post-approval changes.
- Qualification status of relevant equipment and utilities, such as heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems, water, and compressed gases.
- Contractual arrangements relevant to the product, to confirm that they are up to date.

### 4.3 Assess findings and trends

1. Assess the information to determine whether the process remains consistent and whether current specifications for starting materials and finished product remain appropriate.
2. Identify trends and opportunities for product or process improvement.
3. The Report Author selects and interprets trend results using the information available for the product, applying the trend methods appropriate to the data — control charts and run rules where the data allow. A trend is identified for escalation when it crosses an established alert or action level or shows a sustained adverse direction. Statistical tools may support data assessment and decisions.
4. Quality Assurance determines when statistical tools are appropriate.
5. The methods, criteria, and assessments are documented in the report.
6. Base risk assessments on relevant evidence, scientific knowledge, and product and process knowledge, under SOP-QA-009, Quality Risk Management. Match the effort and documentation of the assessment to the uncertainty, importance, and complexity of the issue.

### 4.4 Evaluate and follow up

1. Evaluate the review results to determine whether corrective or preventive action or revalidation should be undertaken.
2. Document the evaluation and resulting decisions.
3. Quality Assurance makes these decisions with the process owners.
4. Manage and review resulting actions on an ongoing basis under SOP-QA-011, Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA), or SOP-QA-004, Change Control, as applicable.
5. The Report Author, with Quality Assurance, determines the pathway from the nature of the action — an investigation or systemic fix follows the CAPA procedure, an alteration to a process, document, or system follows change control, and where both apply, the CAPA owns the investigation and the change implements the fix.
6. Evaluate the effectiveness of the actions.
7. Quality Assurance reviews action effectiveness.
8. Each action is assigned an owner and a due date in FL-EDMS.
9. Each action is tracked by Quality Assurance.
10. Overdue actions are escalated to the QA Manager.

### 4.5 Complete the review

1. The report records the review period and the information reviewed, the trend assessments, the conclusions on process consistency and on whether current specifications remain appropriate, the decisions and actions arising, and any items escalated.
2. The QA Manager approves the report; the review is complete when the report is approved and its actions are recorded in FL-EDMS.
3. Communicate the review conclusions to the process owners and to senior management as an input to management review under SOP-QA-008.

## 5. Records

Annual product quality review records, including the approved reports (APQR-<year>), the compiled information and trend assessments, and the action records arising, retained in FL-EDMS under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
