# SOP-QA-033 — Regulatory Inspection Management

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how Fictitious Labs Inc. prepares for, hosts, responds to, and closes regulatory inspections by the FDA or another authority. It establishes a single, controlled process for inspection conduct, document provision, and written responses to observations.

## 2. Scope

Applies to regulatory inspections by the FDA or another authority at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Assurance, Regulatory Affairs, Quality Control laboratory, Manufacturing. Fictitious Labs Inc. is the biologics license holder for FL-101, regulated by CBER, and the FDA is the regulatory authority for its US products.

Does not cover internal audits or supplier audits. Internal audits follow SOP-QA-007, Internal Audit, and supplier audits follow SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality, not this procedure.

## 3. Responsibilities

**QA Manager**

- Serves as the inspection lead for each inspection.
- Assigns the inspection record number.
- Approves the written response to observations.
- Confirms that inspection commitments are tracked to completion.

**Quality Assurance**

- Hosts inspections by the FDA or another authority.
- Coordinates inspection preparation, hosting, and closure activities.
- Maintains the inspection record in FL-EDMS.
- Tracks commitments that are not managed as a CAPA in the inspection record.

**Regulatory Affairs**

- Serves as the channel for formal communications with the authority.
- Transmits the written response to observations to the authority.
- Records formal communications with the authority in the inspection record.

**Document Control**

- Retrieves controlled documents requested during an inspection.
- Provides controlled documents through the back room process.
- Returns controlled documents to their controlled location after use.

**Function heads**

- Identify subject matter experts for their functions.
- Confirm the availability of subject matter experts during an inspection.
- Review documents from their functions before provision to inspectors.

**Subject matter experts**

- Speak only to their own areas of responsibility and expertise.
- Answer inspector questions within their own areas.
- Refer questions outside their own areas to the QA Manager.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Prepare for an inspection

1. The QA Manager confirms that Quality Assurance will host the inspection.
2. The QA Manager assigns an inspection record number in the format INS-<YYYY>-<NNN>, for example INS-2026-001.
3. Quality Assurance creates the inspection record in FL-EDMS.
4. The QA Manager identifies the personnel required for the front room.
5. The QA Manager identifies the personnel required for the back room.
6. Quality Assurance establishes a front room for inspectors and hosts.
7. Quality Assurance establishes a back room for document retrieval and preparation.
8. Function heads identify subject matter experts for their functions.
9. Quality Assurance confirms that assigned personnel have completed applicable training under SOP-QA-005, Training and Qualification.
10. Quality Assurance prepares a log in FL-EDMS for every document requested and provided.
11. Regulatory Affairs confirms the contact details for formal communications with the authority.
12. The QA Manager briefs front room personnel on this procedure.
13. The QA Manager briefs back room personnel on this procedure.
14. The QA Manager briefs subject matter experts on this procedure.

### 4.2 Host the inspection

1. The QA Manager receives the inspectors on arrival.
2. The QA Manager records the names of the inspectors in the inspection record.
3. The QA Manager records the authority conducting the inspection in the inspection record.
4. The QA Manager records the start date of the inspection in the inspection record.
5. Quality Assurance hosts the inspectors in the front room.
6. The QA Manager introduces the front room personnel to the inspectors.
7. Regulatory Affairs handles formal communications with the authority during the inspection.
8. The QA Manager receives each document request from the inspectors.
9. The QA Manager records each document request in the document log in FL-EDMS.
10. The QA Manager transmits each document request to the back room.
11. Back room personnel retrieve the requested existing record.
12. Back room personnel review the retrieved record for completeness.
13. Function heads review records from their functions before provision.
14. Document Control provides controlled documents in accordance with SOP-QA-003, Document Control.
15. The QA Manager provides the requested record to the inspectors.
16. The QA Manager records each document provided in the document log in FL-EDMS.
17. No personnel create a document during an inspection in response to a request.
18. Personnel provide existing records in response to document requests.
19. Personnel give explanations orally when a record requires explanation.
20. Personnel reserve written explanations for the written response to observations.
21. Subject matter experts answer questions only within their own areas.
22. Subject matter experts refer questions outside their own areas to the QA Manager.
23. The QA Manager records inspection discussions in the inspection record each day.
24. The QA Manager holds a daily review with front room personnel.
25. The QA Manager holds a daily review with back room personnel.
26. The QA Manager records any observations raised by the inspectors in the inspection record.
27. Quality Assurance files inspection notes in the inspection record in FL-EDMS.
28. Personnel prepare documents in accordance with SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.

### 4.3 Respond to observations

1. The QA Manager records the close date of the inspection in the inspection record.
2. The QA Manager records all observations in the inspection record.
3. The QA Manager records any Form FDA 483 items in the inspection record.
4. Quality Assurance assigns an owner for the response to each observation.
5. Each response owner drafts a response to the assigned observation.
6. Each response owner identifies any commitments proposed in the response.
7. Quality Assurance reviews the draft response for accuracy.
8. Regulatory Affairs reviews the draft response before approval.
9. The QA Manager approves the written response to observations.
10. Regulatory Affairs submits the approved written response to the authority.
11. Regulatory Affairs submits the written response within 15 business days of the close of the inspection.
12. Regulatory Affairs records the submission date in the inspection record.
13. Quality Assurance files a copy of the submitted response in the inspection record in FL-EDMS.
14. Quality Assurance opens a CAPA under SOP-QA-011, Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA), where a CAPA is required for a commitment.
15. Quality Assurance tracks each commitment under SOP-QA-011, Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA), where a CAPA is opened.
16. Quality Assurance tracks each remaining commitment in the inspection record where a CAPA is not opened.

### 4.4 Close the inspection and track commitments

1. The QA Manager confirms that the written response has been submitted.
2. Quality Assurance confirms that the document log in FL-EDMS is complete.
3. Quality Assurance confirms that every document requested is recorded in the document log.
4. Quality Assurance confirms that every document provided is recorded in the document log.
5. The QA Manager reviews the inspection record for completeness.
6. Quality Assurance closes the inspection record in FL-EDMS when all required entries are complete.
7. Quality Assurance monitors each commitment tracked in the inspection record to completion.
8. Quality Assurance monitors each commitment tracked under a CAPA to completion.
9. The QA Manager records completion of each commitment in the inspection record.
10. Quality Assurance provides inspection outcomes as a standing input to management review under SOP-QA-008, Management Review.
11. The QA Manager reports open commitments at management review under SOP-QA-008, Management Review.
12. Quality Assurance retains the completed inspection record in FL-EDMS.

## 5. Records

Inspection records are numbered INS-<YYYY>-<NNN>, assigned by Quality Assurance, and held in FL-EDMS. Each inspection record includes the document log of every document requested and provided, inspection notes, any Form FDA 483 items, the approved written response, and the record of commitments and their completion. Inspection records are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
