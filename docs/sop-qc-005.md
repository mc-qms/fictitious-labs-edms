# SOP-QC-005 — Retention (Reserve) Samples

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Control

## 1. Purpose

Establish the retention (reserve) sample program — which samples are kept, in what quantity, under what conditions, and for how long — so that any released lot of FL-101 drug substance can be re-examined after release.

## 2. Scope

Applies to each lot of FL-101 drug substance released by Fictitious Labs Inc.

Does not cover stability samples, which follow SOP-QC-002, Stability Testing, or cell bank vials, which follow SOP-QC-004, Cell Bank Management.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Quality Control**
  - Collect the retention sample for each lot.
  - Maintain the retention sample inventory in FL-EDMS.
  - Store retention samples under this procedure and examine them on schedule.

- **Quality Assurance**
  - Quality Assurance is the Quality Unit function designated for this procedure: Quality Assurance decides whether retention samples are checked or tested in support of an investigation.
  - Approve the destruction of retention samples at the end of the retention period.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Collect the retention sample

1. Collect a sample representative of the lot, drawn from containers across the filling run.
2. Collect at least twice the quantity required to perform every test in SPEC-QC-101, FL-101 Drug Substance Specification.
3. Label each retention container with:
   - The lot number.
   - The container number.
   - The date collected.
   - The collector's initials.
4. Record the sample in the retention sample inventory in FL-EDMS.

### 4.2 Store the retention sample

1. Store retention containers at −20 °C ± 5 °C, the labeled storage condition for FL-101.
2. Segregate retention samples from material in routine use.
3. Restrict access to retention storage to Quality Control and Quality Assurance personnel.

### 4.3 Examine retention samples

1. Examine each retention sample visually at least once a year for evidence of deterioration.
2. Record each examination in the retention sample inventory, including:
   - The date of the examination.
   - The examiner.
   - The result.
3. Report any evidence of deterioration to Quality Assurance as a deviation under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

### 4.4 Use a retention sample in an investigation

1. Quality Assurance decides whether a retention sample is checked or tested, based on whether the examination can confirm a defect, bound the affected population, or inform the risk assessment, under:
   - SOP-QA-013, Complaint Handling.
   - SOP-QA-014, Product Recall.
   - SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
   - SOP-QA-012, Out-of-Specification (OOS) Investigation.
2. Record each removal from retention storage in the inventory, including:
   - The quantity removed.
   - The reason for the removal.
   - The Quality Assurance decision authorizing it.
3. Return any unused portion of the sample to retention storage after the examination.

### 4.5 Retain and destroy

1. Retain each retention sample for at least one year after the lot's expiration date or, where the lot carries a retest date assigned under SOP-QC-002, Stability Testing, one year after that date.
2. Destroy a retention sample only after its retention period has ended.
3. Obtain Quality Assurance approval before each destruction.
4. Record each destruction in the retention sample inventory.

## 5. Records

The retention sample inventory in FL-EDMS; annual examination records; removal and destruction records, retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
