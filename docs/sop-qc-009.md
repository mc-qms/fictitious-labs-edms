# SOP-QC-009 — Pharmaceutical Water and Clean Steam Operations

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Control

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes the routine operation, sanitization, sampling, testing, and trending of pharmaceutical water and clean steam systems at Fictitious Labs Inc. It sets the company action limits for those systems and describes the response when a result reaches an alert or action limit.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the purified water (PW) loop, the water for injection (WFI) loop, and the clean steam generator at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Control laboratory, Quality Assurance, Engineering and Maintenance.

Does not cover qualification of the water and clean steam systems. Qualification of the water and clean steam systems is governed by SOP-QA-026, Equipment, Facilities, Utilities, and Systems Qualification; this procedure is routine operation only.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Analyst**

- Collect water and clean steam condensate samples at the scheduled points of use.
- Perform conductivity and total organic carbon testing on collected samples.
- Record test results in FL-EDMS.

**Laboratory Supervisor**

- Schedule sampling so every required point of use is sampled within its required interval.
- Review completed test results for completeness.
- Verify that results are entered in FL-EDMS.

**Microbiology Supervisor**

- Schedule bioburden and endotoxin testing of collected samples.
- Review bioburden and endotoxin results against the action limits in this procedure.
- Notify Quality Assurance of any action-limit excursion.

**Quality Assurance**

- Approve the action limits stated in this procedure.
- Assess each action-limit excursion.
- Determine whether an excursion is handled under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

**QA Manager**

- Approve changes to the action limits stated in this procedure.
- Review excursion assessments prepared by Quality Assurance.

**Maintenance Technician**

- Operate the PW loop on behalf of Engineering and Maintenance.
- Operate the WFI loop on behalf of Engineering and Maintenance.
- Operate the clean steam generator on behalf of Engineering and Maintenance.
- Perform thermal sanitization of the PW loop.

**Equipment Owner**

- Maintain the operating status of the water and clean steam systems.
- Notify Quality Control of any system shutdown that affects scheduled sampling.
- Notify Quality Assurance of any system fault that affects water or clean steam quality.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Operate and sanitize the loops

1. Operate the PW loop continuously.
2. Operate the WFI loop continuously.
3. Store the WFI loop hot at ≥ 80 °C.
4. Distribute the WFI loop hot at ≥ 80 °C.
5. Monitor the WFI loop distribution temperature.
6. Record the WFI loop distribution temperature in FL-EDMS.
7. Operate the clean steam generator to supply the autoclaves.
8. Operate the clean steam generator to supply sanitization.
9. Sanitize the PW loop thermally at least monthly.
10. Sanitize the PW loop thermally after any action-limit excursion.
11. Record each PW loop sanitization in FL-EDMS.
12. Maintain instruments used for system monitoring under SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance.
13. Process any change to system operation under SOP-QA-004, Change Control.

### 4.2 Sample and test

1. Sample WFI points of use on a rotating schedule.
2. Sample every WFI point of use at least weekly.
3. Sample PW points of use on a rotating schedule.
4. Sample every PW point of use at least monthly.
5. Sample clean steam condensate weekly.
6. Label each collected sample with its point of use.
7. Label each collected sample with its collection date.
8. Deliver collected samples to the Quality Control laboratory.
9. Test PW samples for bioburden.
10. Compare the PW bioburden result against the action limit of 100 CFU/mL.
11. Test WFI samples for bioburden.
12. Compare the WFI bioburden result against the action limit of 10 CFU/100 mL.
13. Test WFI samples for bacterial endotoxin.
14. Compare the WFI endotoxin result against the action limit of 0.125 EU/mL.
15. Note the compendial endotoxin limit of 0.25 EU/mL for comparison.
16. Test water samples for total organic carbon.
17. Compare the total organic carbon result against the action limit of 300 ppb.
18. Note the compendial total organic carbon limit of 500 ppb for comparison.
19. Test water samples for conductivity.
20. Assess the conductivity result against USP <645> Stage 1 limits.
21. Record each test result in FL-EDMS.
22. Document each sampling and testing activity under SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.

### 4.3 Respond to alert and action results

1. Review each result against the applicable action limit.
2. Notify the Laboratory Supervisor of any result approaching an action limit.
3. Notify the Microbiology Supervisor of any bioburden result approaching an action limit.
4. Notify Quality Assurance of any result that reaches an action limit.
5. Identify the affected point of use in the notification to Quality Assurance.
6. Assess the action-limit excursion as directed by Quality Assurance.
7. Handle each confirmed action-limit excursion under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
8. Sanitize the PW loop after a PW action-limit excursion.
9. Resample the affected point of use after sanitization.
10. Retest the resampled point of use for the parameter that exceeded its limit.
11. Raise a corrective or preventive action under SOP-QA-011, Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) when Quality Assurance requires one.
12. Record the excursion response in FL-EDMS.

### 4.4 Trend the results

1. Compile water and clean steam results from FL-EDMS.
2. Trend bioburden results by point of use.
3. Trend endotoxin results by point of use.
4. Trend total organic carbon results by point of use.
5. Trend conductivity results by point of use.
6. Review the trend for any point of use approaching an action limit.
7. Report adverse trends to Quality Assurance.
8. Issue the trend report through FL-EDMS.
9. Review the trend report with the Microbiology Supervisor.
10. File the completed trend report in FL-EDMS.

## 5. Records

Sampling records, test results, sanitization records, excursion notifications and assessments, and trend reports generated under this procedure are recorded in FL-EDMS. Quality Control maintains the sampling schedules and trend reports for the systems covered by this procedure. Records are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
