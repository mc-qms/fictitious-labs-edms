# SOP-QA-048 — Second-Person Verification Rules

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines how second-person verification is performed and recorded.

It sits under SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.

## 2. Perform an independent check

1. Receive the record from the performer.

2. Confirm that the performer has completed the record.

3. Perform the check independently of the performer.

4. For a calculation, compute the result before seeing the performer's result.

5. Compare your computed result with the performer's result.

6. Check only work that you personally witness or check.

7. Do not co-sign work that you did not witness or check.

8. Treat co-signing unwitnessed work as prohibited.

## 3. Record the verification

1. Record the verification in the record being checked.

2. Record the verification at the time you perform the check.

3. Do not reconstruct the verification at a later time.

4. Apply your initials in the record being checked.

5. Use your initials with the meaning defined in SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.

6. Understand that initials in a record mean performed, checked, or approved, as defined in that procedure.

7. Route the completed QC laboratory record in the following order.

8. Have the analyst complete the record first.

9. Have the QC reviewer perform second-person verification second.

10. Route the record to Quality Assurance for review and approval before batch release, per SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices, and SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release.

## 4. Respond when a check disagrees

1. Stop when a check fails.

2. Stop when your result disagrees with the performer's result.

3. Notify your supervisor of the failure or disagreement.

4. Have the performer also notify the supervisor.

5. Do not approve the record while the discrepancy remains unresolved.

6. Investigate a discrepancy found in review under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

7. Complete the investigation before the record is approved.

## 5. Control of this procedure

1. Quality Assurance and the Quality Assurance Supervisor approve this procedure and each revision of it.

2. Make each revision through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
