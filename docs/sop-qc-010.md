# SOP-QC-010 — Compressed Gases and Clean Utilities Monitoring

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Control

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how compressed gases and clean utilities are monitored at Fictitious Labs Inc.
Together with SOP-QC-009, Pharmaceutical Water and Clean Steam Operations, this procedure provides the utility monitoring procedures that the scope of SOP-QC-001, Environmental Monitoring, points to.

## 2. Scope

Applies to compressed gases and clean utilities monitoring at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Control laboratory, Quality Assurance, Manufacturing, Engineering and Maintenance.
The program covers nitrogen, compressed air, carbon dioxide, and oxygen used in production and the QC laboratory.
It covers product-contact points of use and the sampling, testing, review, and trending of results from those points.

Does not cover environmental monitoring of classified areas, water, or clean steam.
Environmental monitoring of classified areas is governed by SOP-QC-001, Environmental Monitoring, whose scope excludes utility monitoring and points it to procedures of its own.
Water and clean steam are governed by SOP-QC-009, Pharmaceutical Water and Clean Steam Operations.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Microbiology Supervisor**
- Own monitoring execution for compressed gases and clean utilities.
- Perform first review of monitoring results.
- Escalate results that reach an action limit to Quality Assurance and Quality Control.
- Confirm that the rotating sampling schedule is completed as planned.
- Confirm that excursions are opened promptly when an action limit is reached.

**Quality Control**
- Set level tables through QC Microbiology for each gas, point of use, and grade of area served.
- Review certificates for bulk and cylinder gases on receipt.
- Test samples for bioburden, particulates, and moisture.
- Test compressed air samples for oil content.
- Make product-impact decisions together with Quality Assurance.

**Quality Assurance**
- Approve level tables set by QC Microbiology.
- Approve monitoring results that require a product-impact decision.
- Make product-impact decisions together with Quality Control.
- Approve the handling of excursions under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

**Maintenance Technician**
- Maintain access to points of use for scheduled sampling.
- Report damage or changes at a point of use to the Microbiology Supervisor.
- Complete repairs assigned after an excursion.
- Confirm that a repaired point of use is returned to its normal configuration.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Sample the points of use

1. Obtain the current rotating sampling schedule from FL-EDMS.
2. Confirm that every product-contact point of use is listed on the schedule.
3. Confirm that each product-contact point is scheduled at least monthly.
4. Check the level table for the gas, point, and grade of area served.
5. Collect the sample containers listed for the scheduled point.
6. Label each container with the gas, point, and date.
7. Go to the point of use at the scheduled time.
8. Record the condition of the point of use before sampling.
9. Collect the bioburden sample from the point.
10. Collect the particulate sample from the point.
11. Collect the moisture sample from the point.
12. Collect an oil content sample when the gas is compressed air.
13. Seal each sample container after collection.
14. Record each sample in the monitoring log at the time of collection.
15. Transfer the samples to QC Microbiology for testing.

### 4.2 Test and review the results

1. Receive the samples into QC Microbiology.
2. Test each sample for bioburden.
3. Test each sample for particulates.
4. Test each sample for moisture.
5. Test each compressed air sample for oil content.
6. Record each test result against the point of use sampled.
7. Compare each result with the level table held in FL-EDMS.
8. Compare bioburden results for product-contact gas delivered to Grade A/B points with the company-set action limit of 1 CFU/m³.
9. Flag any result that reaches an action limit.
10. Pass all results to the Microbiology Supervisor for first review.
11. Review each result against the level table during first review.
12. Sign the first review when it is complete.
13. Pass flagged results to Quality Assurance and Quality Control.
14. Record the completed results in FL-EDMS.

### 4.3 Respond to an excursion

1. Notify Quality Assurance and Quality Control when a result reaches an action limit.
2. Open an excursion record under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
3. Identify the gas and point of use affected by the result.
4. Identify the grade of area served by the affected point.
5. Identify the batches and laboratory work that used the affected point since the last acceptable result.
6. Place the affected information in the excursion record.
7. Assess product impact together with Quality Assurance.
8. Record the product-impact decision in the excursion record.
9. Assign any repair to a Maintenance Technician.
10. Resample the affected point after any repair is complete.
11. Review the resample result before the point returns to routine use.
12. Open a corrective and preventive action under SOP-QA-011, Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA), when the excursion record requires one.
13. Close the excursion record after Quality Assurance approval.

### 4.4 Trend the results

1. Gather the completed results for the trending period.
2. Group the results by gas.
3. Group the results by point of use.
4. Group the results by grade of area served.
5. Compare the grouped results with the results from the prior period.
6. Note any point with repeated results near an action limit.
7. Note any gas with a change in results over time.
8. Record the trend findings in FL-EDMS.
9. Pass the trend findings to Quality Assurance and Quality Control.
10. Raise any adverse trend under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
11. Review the level tables when a trend shows they need attention.
12. Obtain Quality Assurance approval for any change to a level table.

### 4.5 Receive bulk and cylinder gases

1. Receive each bulk or cylinder gas delivery against its certificate.
2. Pass the certificate to Quality Control for review.
3. Review the certificate under SOP-QC-008, Raw Material Sampling, Testing, and TSE/BSE Control.
4. Review the supplier information under SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality.
5. Record the certificate review in FL-EDMS.
6. Release accepted gas for use in production and the QC laboratory.
7. Escalate any certificate that does not match the delivery to Quality Assurance.

## 5. Records

Records made under this procedure include sampling schedules, monitoring logs, test results, first reviews, trend findings, level tables, and certificate reviews.
Level tables are set by QC Microbiology and approved by Quality Assurance and held in FL-EDMS, on the same pattern as SOP-QC-001, Environmental Monitoring.
Excursion records and product-impact decisions are kept with the monitoring results for the affected point of use.
All records are completed and kept under SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
Records are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
