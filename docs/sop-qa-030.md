# SOP-QA-030 — Pest Control and Facility Sanitation

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how the pest control program is operated and how sanitation of non-classified areas is maintained at Fictitious Labs Inc.
It defines how pest evidence is reported, assessed, and trended so that GMP areas are protected from pest activity.

## 2. Scope

Applies to pest control and to facility sanitation of non-classified areas at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Assurance, Manufacturing, Warehouse.

Does not cover cleaning and disinfection of classified areas. Cleaning and disinfection of classified areas is governed by SOP-QA-034, Routine Facility Cleaning, Sanitization, and Sporicidal Rotation, not this procedure.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Quality Assurance**

- Own the pest control program.
- Review contractor service reports.
- Trend the contractor's findings.
- Assess pest evidence that may affect a GMP area.
- Determine whether pest evidence requires handling under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

**QA Manager**

- Approve the site map for monitoring devices and traps.
- Review trends of contractor findings.
- Review assessments of pest evidence affecting a GMP area.
- Approve changes to the pest control program.

**Warehouse Supervisor**

- Maintain sanitation schedules for warehouse areas.
- Confirm completion of scheduled sanitation in warehouse areas.
- Report pest evidence observed in warehouse areas to Quality Assurance.
- Restrict access to an affected warehouse area when directed by Quality Assurance.

**Manufacturing Supervisor**

- Report pest evidence observed in manufacturing support areas to Quality Assurance.
- Confirm completion of scheduled sanitation in manufacturing support areas.
- Restrict access to an affected support area when directed by Quality Assurance.
- Provide area access for scheduled contractor service visits.

**Pest-Control Contractor**

- Perform service visits as the licensed pest-control contractor.
- Service monitoring devices and traps at the locations shown on the site map.
- Record findings from each service visit.
- Report evidence of infestation to Quality Assurance.
- Apply pesticide only outside classified production areas.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Operate the pest control program

1. Quality Assurance maintains the pest control program.
2. Quality Assurance qualifies the pest-control contractor as a supplier under SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality.
3. The pest-control contractor performs service visits monthly.
4. Quality Assurance holds the site map for monitoring devices and traps in FL-EDMS.
5. The pest-control contractor places monitoring devices to the site map.
6. The pest-control contractor places traps to the site map.
7. The pest-control contractor inspects each monitoring device at each service visit.
8. The pest-control contractor inspects each trap at each service visit.
9. The pest-control contractor records the condition of each device inspected.
10. The pest-control contractor records the condition of each trap inspected.
11. The pest-control contractor records all findings from the service visit.
12. The pest-control contractor submits a service report for each visit.
13. Quality Assurance reviews each service report.
14. Quality Assurance trends the contractor's findings.
15. Quality Assurance files each reviewed service report in FL-EDMS.
16. The pest-control contractor applies no pesticide inside a classified production area.
17. Quality Assurance reviews proposed changes to device locations against the site map.
18. Quality Assurance updates the site map in FL-EDMS after an approved change.

### 4.2 Respond to pest evidence

1. The person observing pest evidence reports the observation to Quality Assurance.
2. The person observing pest evidence records the location of the observation.
3. The person observing pest evidence records the date of the observation.
4. Quality Assurance logs the reported observation.
5. Quality Assurance notifies the supervisor of the affected area.
6. Quality Assurance requests an assessment visit from the pest-control contractor.
7. The pest-control contractor inspects the affected area.
8. The pest-control contractor records the findings of the inspection.
9. Quality Assurance reviews the contractor's findings.
10. Quality Assurance determines whether a GMP area is affected.
11. Quality Assurance handles evidence of infestation affecting a GMP area under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
12. Quality Assurance determines whether corrective and preventive action is required under SOP-QA-011, Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA).
13. The supervisor of the affected area restricts access to the area when directed by Quality Assurance.
14. The pest-control contractor completes the treatment directed by Quality Assurance outside classified production areas.
15. Quality Assurance verifies completion of the directed treatment.
16. Quality Assurance includes the findings in the trend of contractor findings.
17. Quality Assurance closes the log entry after verification is complete.

### 4.3 Maintain sanitation of non-classified areas

1. Each owning function maintains a sanitation schedule for its non-classified areas.
2. Warehouse maintains the sanitation schedule for warehouse areas per SOP-QA-023, Warehouse and Distribution Control.
3. The owning function lists warehouse areas on the applicable schedule.
4. The owning function lists corridors on the applicable schedule.
5. The owning function lists waste staging areas on the applicable schedule.
6. The owning function lists locker areas on the applicable schedule.
7. The owning function lists support areas on the applicable schedule.
8. Assigned personnel perform sanitation at the frequency stated on the schedule.
9. Assigned personnel record completion of each scheduled sanitation activity.
10. The supervisor of the owning function reviews each completion record.
11. The supervisor of the owning function reports a missed scheduled activity to Quality Assurance.
12. Quality Assurance reviews reported missed activities.
13. Assigned personnel remove waste from waste staging areas at the frequency stated on the schedule.
14. Assigned personnel keep corridor areas clear of stored materials after sanitation.
15. Assigned personnel keep locker areas clean after sanitation.
16. The owning function updates its sanitation schedule when an area use changes.
17. Quality Assurance reviews changes to sanitation schedules for non-classified areas.
18. Personnel refer cleaning of classified production areas to SOP-QA-034, Routine Facility Cleaning, Sanitization, and Sporicidal Rotation.
19. Personnel consider pest exclusion and sanitation requirements for classified areas under SOP-QA-029, Contamination Control Strategy.

## 5. Records

Records generated under this procedure include contractor service reports, the site map for monitoring devices and traps held in FL-EDMS, pest evidence logs, trend reviews of contractor findings, and sanitation completion records for non-classified areas. Each record is completed and maintained in accordance with good documentation practices. Records are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
