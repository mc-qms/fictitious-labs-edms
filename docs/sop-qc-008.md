# SOP-QC-008 — Raw Material Sampling, Testing, and TSE/BSE Control

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Control

## 1. Purpose

Establish how raw materials are sampled, tested, and released for use at Fictitious Labs Inc. Establish the controls for reduced testing and for materials of animal origin, including TSE/BSE risk assessment.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the receipt, sampling, testing, and release for use of raw materials used in the manufacture of FL-101 at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Control laboratory, Quality Assurance, Warehouse.

Does not cover the creation or approval of specifications, which follows SOP-QA-028, Specification Management. Does not cover the validation of analytical procedures, which follows SOP-QC-003, Analytical Procedure Validation. Does not cover analytical method transfer or compendial verification, which follows SOP-QC-007, Analytical Method Transfer and Compendial Verification. Does not cover the qualification of suppliers, which follows SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Analyst**
  - Sample each assigned lot under Section 4.1.
  - Perform the tests assigned from the material specification.
  - Record each result in FL-EDMS.
  - Report any failing result to the Laboratory Supervisor.

- **Laboratory Supervisor**
  - Assign sampling to an Analyst.
  - Assign testing to an Analyst.
  - Review completed test records for completeness.
  - Route completed test records to Quality Control for the release decision.

- **Quality Control**
  - Determine the sampling for each material from its specification.
  - Determine the testing for each material from its specification.
  - Release material for use.
  - Maintain the animal-derived materials register in FL-EDMS.

- **Quality Assurance**
  - Approve each written justification for reduced testing.
  - Approve each TSE/BSE risk assessment.
  - Decide the disposition of a material that fails its specification under SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality.

- **QA Manager**
  - Approve a TSE/BSE risk assessment where Quality Assurance escalates the assessment for management approval.

- **Warehouse Operator**
  - Receive each delivery of raw material under SOP-QA-023, Warehouse and Distribution Control.
  - Place each received lot in Quarantine status under SOP-QA-023, Warehouse and Distribution Control.
  - Notify Quality Control of each received lot.

- **Warehouse Supervisor**
  - Verify Quarantine segregation of each received lot.
  - Change the status of a lot in FL-EDMS after Quality Control releases the material for use.
  - Change the status of a lot in FL-EDMS after Quality Assurance directs rejection of the material.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Sample the received lot

1. Receive notification of a received lot from the Warehouse Operator.
2. Confirm the lot is in Quarantine status in FL-EDMS.
3. Retrieve the material specification for the lot.
4. Read the sampling plan in the material specification.
5. Determine the sampling for the lot from the specification.
6. Assign the sampling to an Analyst.
7. Collect the sample from the lot under the sampling plan in the specification.
8. Sample each lot received under that sampling plan.
9. Label each sample container with the material name.
10. Label each sample container with the lot number.
11. Label each sample container with the sampling date.
12. Record the sampling in FL-EDMS.
13. Transfer the sample to the Quality Control laboratory.
14. Retain the lot in Quarantine status pending test results.

### 4.2 Test and release the material

1. Retrieve the material specification for the sampled lot.
2. Determine the testing for the lot from the specification.
3. Assign an identity test for the lot.
4. Perform at least an identity test on each lot received.
5. Assign the remaining testing stated in the specification.
6. Perform each assigned test under the applicable test method.
7. Use reference standards controlled under SOP-QC-006, Reference Standards and Laboratory Controls.
8. Record each test result in FL-EDMS.
9. Compare each test result against the specification.
10. Review the completed test record.
11. Release the material for use in FL-EDMS where each result meets the specification.
12. Notify Warehouse of each release for use.
13. Handle a material that fails its specification under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
14. Reject a material that fails its specification.
15. Change the rejected lot to Rejected status under SOP-QA-023, Warehouse and Distribution Control.
16. Route the rejected lot for disposition under SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality.

### 4.3 Apply reduced testing where justified

1. Identify a material proposed for reduced testing.
2. Retrieve the supplier qualification record under SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality.
3. Review the supplier history for the material under SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality.
4. Prepare a written justification for the proposed reduced testing.
5. State the tests proposed for reduction in the justification.
6. State the basis in supplier qualification in the justification.
7. State the basis in supplier history in the justification.
8. Submit the written justification to Quality Assurance.
9. Obtain Quality Assurance approval of the justification before applying reduced testing.
10. Apply reduced testing only to the tests named in the approved justification.
11. Perform identity testing on each lot received.
12. Do not reduce identity testing under any justification.
13. Perform the full testing of the specification where no approved justification is in place.
14. Review each approved justification when the supplier history changes.
15. Withdraw reduced testing where Quality Assurance withdraws its approval.

### 4.4 Control materials of animal origin (TSE/BSE)

1. Identify each material of animal origin at receipt.
2. Enter each material of animal origin in the animal-derived materials register in FL-EDMS.
3. Maintain the animal-derived materials register in FL-EDMS.
4. Treat the cell culture media used for the FL-101 CHO-derived production cell line as a principal animal-origin concern.
5. Treat the supplements used for the FL-101 CHO-derived production cell line as a principal animal-origin concern.
6. Confirm FL-101 is a monoclonal antibody drug substance expressed in a CHO-derived cell line.
7. Retrieve the supplier TSE/BSE certification for the material.
8. File the supplier TSE/BSE certification under SOP-QA-015, Supplier Quality.
9. Prepare a TSE/BSE risk assessment for the material.
10. Follow the risk categorization approach of EMA/410/01 in the assessment.
11. Submit the TSE/BSE risk assessment to Quality Assurance.
12. Obtain Quality Assurance approval of the TSE/BSE risk assessment.
13. Withhold the material from use until Quality Assurance has approved the assessment.
14. Withhold the material from use until the supplier TSE/BSE certification is on file.
15. Release the material for use only after each condition in this section is met.
16. Update the animal-derived materials register when a new material of animal origin is introduced.
17. Update the animal-derived materials register when a supplier of a listed material changes.
18. Reassess the TSE/BSE risk when a supplier of a listed material changes.

## 5. Records

Sampling records, test records, material release records, written justifications for reduced testing, TSE/BSE risk assessments, supplier TSE/BSE certifications, and the animal-derived materials register in FL-EDMS are the records generated under this procedure, retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
