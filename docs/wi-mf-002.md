# WI-MF-002 — Line Clearance and Room/Equipment Turnaround

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Manufacturing

## 1. Purpose

This instruction describes how to perform and verify line clearance in manufacturing rooms and on manufacturing equipment before a batch or campaign begins. It sits under SOP-QA-027, Batch Record Content and Control, and SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills.

## 2. Perform line clearance

1. Confirm that the previous batch or campaign in the room has ended.
2. Obtain the line clearance checklist in the batch record for the next batch.
3. Confirm that the batch record was issued under SOP-QA-027, Batch Record Content and Control.
4. Confirm that the checklist follows FRM-MF-101, Master Batch Record, FL-101 Drug Substance.
5. Remove all materials of the previous batch from the room.
6. Remove all materials of the previous batch from the equipment.
7. Remove all labels of the previous batch from the room.
8. Remove all labels of the previous batch from the equipment.
9. Remove all records of the previous batch from the room.
10. Remove all records of the previous batch from the equipment.
11. Check the room for any remaining item of the previous batch.
12. Check each item of equipment for any remaining item of the previous batch.
13. Confirm that the room status is correct for the next batch.
14. Confirm that the equipment status is correct for the next batch.
15. Confirm that only one cell line is to be handled in the suite for the next batch.
16. Perform line clearance between cell lines when the cell line handled in the suite changes, per SOP-QC-004, Cell Bank Management.
17. Record each completed checklist item in the batch record.
18. Make each record entry under SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
19. Sign and date the completed checklist in the batch record.
20. Present the completed checklist to the second person for verification.
21. Stop the start of the batch if any checklist item fails.
22. Notify the Manufacturing Supervisor if any checklist item fails.
23. Notify Quality Assurance if any checklist item fails.

## 3. Verify and release the room for the next batch

1. Give the completed line clearance checklist to a second person for verification.
2. Have the second person check the room against the completed checklist.
3. Have the second person check each item of equipment against the completed checklist.
4. Have the second person confirm that no materials of the previous batch remain in the room.
5. Have the second person confirm that no labels of the previous batch remain in the room.
6. Have the second person confirm that no records of the previous batch remain in the room.
7. Have the second person confirm that no materials of the previous batch remain on the equipment.
8. Have the second person confirm that no labels of the previous batch remain on the equipment.
9. Have the second person confirm that no records of the previous batch remain on the equipment.
10. Have the second person confirm that the room status is correct for the next batch.
11. Have the second person confirm that the equipment status is correct for the next batch.
12. Have the second person sign and date the checklist in the batch record.
13. Release the room and equipment for the next batch only after the second person has signed the checklist.
14. Do not start the batch if the clearance has failed.
15. Notify the Manufacturing Supervisor of a failed clearance.
16. Notify Quality Assurance of a failed clearance.
17. Handle a product impact from a failed clearance under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
18. Use SOP-QA-022, Labeling and Packaging Control, for packaging line clearance.
19. Apply this instruction only to manufacturing rooms and equipment.

## 4. Control of this instruction

1. Quality Assurance and the Manufacturing Supervisor approve this instruction and each revision of it.
2. Make each revision through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
