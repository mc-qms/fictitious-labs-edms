# SOP-QA-038 — Signature Log, Authorized Signatories, and Delegation of Authority

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

This procedure establishes the signature log for handwritten signatures and initials on GMP records at Fictitious Labs Inc. It defines who may sign and approve records and how approval authority may be delegated.

## 2. Scope

Applies to handwritten signatures and initials on GMP records at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Assurance, Quality Control laboratory, Manufacturing, Regulatory Affairs, Document Control.

Does not cover electronic signatures. Electronic signatures in FL-EDMS follow SOP-QA-018, Computer System Validation and Data Integrity; this procedure covers handwritten signatures and initials and the delegation of approval authority.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Document Control**

- Maintains the signature log in FL-EDMS.
- Adds entries to the signature log when notified that a person is qualified to sign.
- Removes entries from the signature log when a person leaves or changes function.
- Records delegations of approval authority in FL-EDMS.
- Makes the current signature log available for verification during record review.

**Quality Assurance**

- Verifies that signatures and initials on reviewed records match entries in the signature log.
- Verifies that approvals were given by the person holding the approval authority or by a recorded delegate.
- Handles signatures or initials from persons not in the log under section 4.4 of this procedure.

**QA Manager**

- Holds approval authority for decisions assigned to the QA Manager by other procedures.
- Makes delegations of Quality Assurance approval authority.
- Remains accountable for decisions made by a delegate under a delegation the QA Manager issued.

**Function supervisors**

- Notify Document Control when a person in their function becomes qualified to sign records.
- Notify Document Control when a person in their function leaves or changes function.
- Confirm the scope of records each person in their function is qualified to sign.
- Ensure persons in their function sign only records within their qualified scope.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Maintain the signature log

1. Document Control creates one signature log entry for each authorized person in FL-EDMS.
2. Document Control records the person's name in the entry.
3. Document Control records the person's specimen signature in the entry.
4. Document Control records the person's initials in the entry.
5. Document Control records the person's function in the entry.
6. Document Control records the period the entry is effective in the entry.
7. The function supervisor confirms the person's training under SOP-QA-005, Training and Qualification, qualifies the person for the records the person will sign.
8. Document Control adds the person to the log only after the confirmation in step 7 is received.
9. Document Control removes the entry when the person leaves the company.
10. Document Control removes the entry when the person changes function.
11. Document Control records a new entry when a person changes function and is qualified to sign in the new function.
12. Document Control keeps superseded entries retrievable in FL-EDMS for verification of records signed during the period the entry was effective.

### 4.2 Sign and approve records

1. The signer confirms their own entry is current in the signature log before signing a GMP record.
2. The signer signs the record using the specimen signature recorded in the log.
3. The signer initials the record using the initials recorded in the log.
4. The signer applies initials only with the meaning defined in the documentation conventions for the record: performed, checked, or approved.
5. The signer signs only records the signer is qualified to sign.
6. A reviewer accepts a handwritten entry on a GMP record only from a person in the log.
7. A reviewer compares the signature or initials on the record to the specimen in the log.
8. A reviewer confirms the log entry was effective on the date the record was signed.
9. An approver gives an approval only when the approver holds the approval authority for that record.
10. An approver who does not hold the approval authority does not approve the record.
11. A delegate approves a record only within the scope of a delegation recorded under section 4.3.
12. The signer dates each signature or initials entry at the time of signing.

### 4.3 Delegate approval authority

1. The delegator prepares the delegation in writing.
2. The delegator names the delegate in the delegation.
3. The delegator states the scope of the delegation.
4. The delegator states the start date of the delegation.
5. The delegator states the end date of the delegation.
6. The delegator signs the delegation.
7. Document Control records the delegation in FL-EDMS.
8. The delegate relies on the delegation only after Document Control has recorded it in FL-EDMS.
9. The delegate approves records only within the stated scope of the delegation.
10. The delegate approves records only within the stated period of the delegation.
11. The delegator remains accountable for decisions made under the delegation.
12. The QA Manager makes each delegation of Quality Assurance approval authority.
13. The QA Manager delegates a decision that SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release, reserves to the QA Manager only to a named Quality Assurance manager-level delegate.
14. The QA Manager delegates a decision that SOP-QA-014, Product Recall, reserves to the QA Manager only to a named Quality Assurance manager-level delegate.
15. Document Control removes the delegation record from current status in FL-EDMS when the delegation period ends.
16. The delegator withdraws the delegation in writing if the delegation must end before its stated end date.
17. Document Control records a withdrawal under step 16 in FL-EDMS before the withdrawal is relied on.

### 4.4 Respond to an unauthorized signature

1. The person who identifies the signature or initials stops further processing of the affected record.
2. The person who identifies the signature or initials notifies Quality Assurance.
3. Quality Assurance compares the signature or initials to the signature log in FL-EDMS.
4. Quality Assurance checks FL-EDMS for a delegation covering the signature or approval.
5. Quality Assurance confirms whether the person was in the log on the date the record was signed.
6. Quality Assurance confirms whether the entry was within the scope of a recorded delegation, where a delegation applies.
7. Quality Assurance handles a signature or initials from a person not in the log under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
8. Quality Assurance handles a signature or initials outside a delegation's scope under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
9. Quality Assurance documents the outcome of the checks in steps 3 through 6 in the record of the handling under step 7 or step 8, as applicable.

## 5. Records

The signature log, including superseded entries, and records of delegation and withdrawal of approval authority are maintained in FL-EDMS by Document Control. These records provide the basis for verifying signatures and initials on GMP records, including records reviewed under SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release. Signature log entries and delegation records are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
