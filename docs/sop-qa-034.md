# SOP-QA-034 — Routine Facility Cleaning, Sanitization, and Sporicidal Rotation

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes the routine cleaning, sanitization, and sporicidal rotation of classified production areas at Fictitious Labs Inc. It provides the floor-level execution steps for the cleaning and disinfection program described in Section 4.

## 2. Scope

Applies to routine cleaning, sanitization, and sporicidal rotation of the classified production areas designated Grade B, Grade C, and Grade D, with Grade A critical zones, at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Manufacturing, Quality Control laboratory, Quality Assurance.

Does not cover cleaning validation or sanitation of non-classified areas; cleaning validation is governed by SOP-QA-017, Cleaning Validation, and sanitation of non-classified areas by SOP-QA-030, Pest Control and Facility Sanitation, not this procedure.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Manufacturing Operator**

- Perform cleaning and disinfection tasks assigned for the area.
- Follow the approved agents and use conditions stated in the cleaning and disinfection program.
- Record completed work in the area cleaning record in FL-EDMS.
- Report a missed task to the Manufacturing Supervisor.

**Manufacturing Supervisor**

- Assign cleaning and disinfection tasks to trained area personnel.
- Confirm completion of scheduled tasks for the area.
- Review the area cleaning record for the area.
- Escalate an unresolved cleaning problem to Quality Assurance.

**Microbiology Supervisor**

- Support QC Microbiology in maintaining the cleaning and disinfection program.
- Review the sporicidal rotation schedule for Grade B, Grade C, and Grade D areas.
- Evaluate a cleaning concern raised by area personnel.
- Communicate a program change to affected area personnel.

**Quality Control**

- Maintain the cleaning and disinfection program through QC Microbiology under SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills.
- Specify the approved agents in the cleaning and disinfection program.
- Specify the rotation including a sporicide in the cleaning and disinfection program.
- Perform environmental monitoring of classified areas under SOP-QC-001, Environmental Monitoring.

**Quality Assurance**

- Approve the cleaning and disinfection program.
- Approve changes to the cleaning and disinfection program.
- Review deviations raised under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
- Confirm that this procedure remains consistent with SOP-QA-029, Contamination Control Strategy.

## 4. Procedure

This procedure is the floor-level execution of the cleaning and disinfection program that SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills, assigns to QC Microbiology to maintain and Quality Assurance to approve. The contamination control strategy of SOP-QA-029, Contamination Control Strategy, is the governing policy for this procedure.

### 4.1 Prepare to clean

Complete each preparation step before starting work in the area.

1. Confirm completion of training under SOP-QA-005, Training and Qualification.
2. Gown in accordance with SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills.
3. Identify the grade of the area scheduled for cleaning.
4. Review the scheduled task for the area in the cleaning and disinfection program.
5. Select the approved agent specified in the program for the scheduled task.
6. Check the use conditions stated in the program for the selected agent.
7. Check the in-use period stated in the program for the selected agent.
8. Discard an agent after the end of its stated in-use period.
9. Select cleaning tools dedicated to the grade of the area.
10. Inspect each selected cleaning tool before use.
11. Remove a damaged cleaning tool from service.
12. Confirm the area cleaning record for the area is available in FL-EDMS.

### 4.2 Clean and disinfect by grade

Clean each surface before disinfection, because residues and soil are removed before a disinfectant is applied.

1. Disinfect Grade B surfaces before each production session.
2. Disinfect Grade C areas on each day of operation.
3. Disinfect Grade D areas weekly.
4. Remove visible residues from the surface.
5. Remove soil from the surface.
6. Apply the cleaning agent specified in the program to the surface.
7. Wipe the surface with a cleaning tool dedicated to the grade of the area.
8. Remove the cleaning agent residue from the surface.
9. Inspect the cleaned surface.
10. Repeat the cleaning steps for a surface with remaining visible residue.
11. Apply the disinfectant specified in the program to the cleaned surface.
12. Hold the disinfectant on the surface for the wet contact time stated in the program.
13. Keep the surface wet throughout the stated wet contact time.
14. Leave Grade A critical zones to personnel qualified for work in those zones under SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills.
15. Keep cleaning tools dedicated to one grade within that grade.
16. Prohibit movement of a cleaning tool to a cleaner grade.

### 4.3 Apply the sporicidal rotation

Apply a sporicidal agent at the frequency stated in the cleaning and disinfection program for the grade of the area.

1. Review the sporicidal rotation in the cleaning and disinfection program.
2. Identify the sporicidal agent specified in the program for the scheduled rotation.
3. Check the use conditions stated in the program for the sporicidal agent.
4. Check the in-use period stated in the program for the sporicidal agent.
5. Clean the surface before application of the sporicidal agent.
6. Apply a sporicidal agent at least monthly in Grade B areas.
7. Apply a sporicidal agent at least monthly in Grade C areas.
8. Apply a sporicidal agent at least quarterly in Grade D areas.
9. Apply the sporicidal agent to the surface specified in the rotation.
10. Hold the sporicidal agent on the surface for the wet contact time stated in the program.
11. Keep the surface wet throughout the stated wet contact time.
12. Use only cleaning tools dedicated to the grade of the area for the application.

### 4.4 Record the work and respond to problems

Record the work in the area cleaning record in FL-EDMS on completion of the task.

1. Open the area cleaning record in FL-EDMS.
2. Record the area cleaned in the area cleaning record.
3. Record the grade of the area in the area cleaning record.
4. Record the agent used in the area cleaning record.
5. Record the date of the task in the area cleaning record.
6. Record completion of the sporicidal rotation in the area cleaning record.
7. Sign the completed entry in the area cleaning record.
8. Report a missed scheduled task to the Manufacturing Supervisor.
9. Report a surface with remaining visible residue to the Manufacturing Supervisor.
10. Stop disinfection of a surface with remaining visible residue.
11. Raise a deviation under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations, for a departure from this procedure.
12. Notify Quality Assurance of a departure from the approved program.
13. Follow SOP-QC-001, Environmental Monitoring, for environmental monitoring of the area.
14. Present the area cleaning record to the Manufacturing Supervisor for review.

## 5. Records

The area cleaning record in FL-EDMS is the record of cleaning, disinfection, and sporicidal rotation performed under this procedure. Each entry identifies the area, its grade, the agent used, and the date of the task, and the entry is signed by the person who performed the work.

A departure from this procedure is documented under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations. Records generated under this procedure are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
