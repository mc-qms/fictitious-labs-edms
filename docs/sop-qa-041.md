# SOP-QA-041 — Sterilizing-Grade Filter Integrity Testing

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how sterilizing-grade filters are integrity-tested before use and after use at Fictitious Labs Inc. It describes how test results are recorded and how a failed integrity test is handled.

## 2. Scope

Applies to sterilizing-grade filter integrity testing at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Manufacturing, Quality Assurance, Quality Control laboratory.

Does not cover filter selection or the filtration step itself. Filter selection and the filtration step itself are governed by SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills, and single-use filter assemblies are governed by SOP-QA-032, Single-Use Systems and Extractables/Leachables; this procedure governs integrity testing only.

Sterilizing-grade filters are 0.2 µm maximum nominal pore size, per SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills.

Every sterilizing-grade filter is integrity-tested after use.

A filter is also tested before use, after sterilization (PUPSIT), where the contamination control strategy requires it.

SOP-QA-029, Contamination Control Strategy, states the policy at strategy level.

SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and Media Fills, requires integrity testing after use and before use after sterilization where the CCS requires it.

This procedure is the testing procedure itself.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Manufacturing Operator**

- Perform integrity testing as described in this procedure.
- Record test results in the batch record.
- Stop use of a filter that has failed a pre-use test.
- Notify the Manufacturing Supervisor of a failed integrity test.

**Manufacturing Supervisor**

- Confirm that integrity testing is performed as described in this procedure.
- Confirm that a filter that has failed a pre-use test is not used.
- Confirm that a replacement filter is sterilized and tested before filtration begins.
- Notify Quality Assurance of a failed integrity test.

**Quality Assurance**

- Assess product impact following a failed post-use integrity test.
- Determine disposition under SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release.
- Confirm that a failed post-use test is handled as a deviation under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
- Confirm that test results are recorded in the batch record.

**QA Manager**

- Review the assessment of a failed post-use integrity test.
- Approve the disposition determined under SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release.
- Confirm that the deviation handling under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations, is complete.

## 4. Procedure

Testing is by bubble point or diffusive flow.

Testing is performed against the limit validated for the filter in its qualification under SOP-QA-026, Equipment, Facilities, Utilities, and Systems Qualification.

Integrity test apparatus is calibrated under SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance.

Testers are trained under SOP-QA-005, Training and Qualification.

### 4.1 Test a filter before use (PUPSIT)

1. Confirm that the contamination control strategy under SOP-QA-029, Contamination Control Strategy, requires a pre-use test for the filter.

2. Confirm that the filter has been sterilized.

3. Confirm that the integrity test apparatus is calibrated under SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance.

4. Confirm that the tester is trained under SOP-QA-005, Training and Qualification.

5. Connect the filter to the integrity test apparatus.

6. Perform the test by bubble point or diffusive flow.

7. Compare the result against the limit validated for the filter in its qualification under SOP-QA-026, Equipment, Facilities, Utilities, and Systems Qualification.

8. Record the result in the batch record.

9. Proceed to Section 4.3 if the filter fails the test.

### 4.2 Test a filter after use

1. Confirm that filtration is complete.

2. Confirm that the integrity test apparatus is calibrated under SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance.

3. Confirm that the tester is trained under SOP-QA-005, Training and Qualification.

4. Connect the filter to the integrity test apparatus.

5. Perform the test by bubble point or diffusive flow.

6. Compare the result against the limit validated for the filter in its qualification under SOP-QA-026, Equipment, Facilities, Utilities, and Systems Qualification.

7. Record the result in the batch record.

8. Proceed to Section 4.3 if the filter fails the test.

A filter that passes post-use testing supports release of the filtered batch under SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release.

### 4.3 Respond to a failed integrity test

1. Stop use of a filter that has failed a pre-use test.

2. Replace a filter that has failed a pre-use test.

3. Sterilize the replacement filter.

4. Test the replacement filter before filtration begins.

5. Record the failed pre-use result in the batch record.

6. Raise a deviation under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations, for a failed post-use test.

7. Notify Quality Assurance of a failed post-use test.

8. Assess product impact for a batch filtered through a filter whose post-use test failed.

9. Determine disposition under SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release.

10. Hold release of the batch until Quality Assurance completes its assessment.

A batch filtered through a filter whose post-use test failed is not released until Quality Assurance completes its assessment.

### 4.4 Record the results

1. Record the test method used in the batch record.

2. Record the test result in the batch record.

3. Record the identity of the filter tested in the batch record.

4. Record the identity of the tester in the batch record.

5. Record a failed result in the batch record.

6. Link a failed post-use result to the deviation raised under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

Results are recorded in the batch record.

## 5. Records

Results of sterilizing-grade filter integrity testing are recorded in the batch record for the filtered batch. The batch record includes the test method used, the result obtained, the identity of the filter tested, and the identity of the tester. A failed post-use result is also linked to the deviation raised under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations, and to the Quality Assurance assessment and disposition completed under SOP-QA-006, Batch Record Review and Product Release. Records created under this procedure are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
