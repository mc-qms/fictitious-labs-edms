# SOP-QA-049 — Visitor/Contractor Escort and Clock Change Adjustment

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines how visitors and contractors are badged and escorted in GMP areas, and how standalone instrument clocks are adjusted after a daylight saving time change.

It sits under SOP-QA-005, Training and Qualification, SOP-QA-018, Computer System Validation and Data Integrity, and SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.

## 2. Badge and escort a visitor or contractor

1. Badge each visitor at entry.

2. Badge each contractor at entry.

3. Assign a trained escort to the visitor or contractor.

4. Confirm that the escort is trained under SOP-QA-005, Training and Qualification.

5. Escort the visitor or contractor at all times in GMP areas.

6. Do not leave a visitor or contractor unescorted in a GMP area.

7. Escort an unescorted visitor found in a GMP area out of the area.

8. Report an unescorted visitor event to Quality Assurance on the same business day.

9. Do not connect a personal storage device, such as a USB drive, to a GMP workstation.

10. Govern system access under SOP-QA-018, Computer System Validation and Data Integrity.

## 3. Adjust standalone clocks after a time change

1. Identify each daylight saving time change.

2. Have the Equipment Owner check the clocks of standalone instruments after the change.

3. Complete the check within 2 business days of the change.

4. Check balances that are not synchronized to network time.

5. Check particle counters that are not synchronized to network time.

6. Check chromatography systems that are not synchronized to network time.

7. Adjust each clock where adjustment is needed.

8. Follow equipment ownership and the equipment register under SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance.

9. Identify any overlapping timestamps created in a record by the clock change.

10. Identify any ambiguous timestamps created in a record by the clock change.

11. Annotate overlapping or ambiguous timestamps in the record under SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.

12. Do not use a single line-through correction to alter a timestamp.

13. Add an explanatory note to the record for the affected timestamp.

14. Initial the explanatory note.

15. Date the explanatory note.

## 4. Control of this procedure

1. Quality Assurance and the Quality Assurance Supervisor approve this procedure and each revision of it.

2. Make each revision through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
