# SOP-QA-050 — Bound Logbook Issuance, Use, and Review

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

Govern the issuance, use, review, and closure of bound logbooks, so that handwritten operational records are attributable, legible, complete, and fully accounted for at the end of each logbook's life.

## 2. Scope

Applies to every bound logbook used at Fictitious Labs Inc. — equipment use logs, room logs, cleaning logs, and instrument logs — in Manufacturing, Quality Control, Quality Assurance, and Engineering and Maintenance.

Does not cover batch records, which follow SOP-QA-027, Batch Record Content and Control; controlled forms, which follow SOP-QA-003, Document Control; or electronic records in FL-EDMS, which follow SOP-QA-018, Computer System Validation and Data Integrity.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Document Control**
  - Issue logbooks and record each issuance in the logbook register in FL-EDMS.
  - Reconcile and archive closed logbooks.
- **Owning Function Supervisor**
  - Review the function's active logbooks monthly.
  - Confirm that entries follow section 4.2.
- **Quality Assurance**
  - Review each closure reconciliation before the logbook is archived.
  - Open a deviation for a lost or damaged logbook under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Issue a logbook

1. Request a logbook from Document Control, stating the equipment, room, or activity it will record and the owning function.
2. Document Control assigns the next identifier from the logbook register, in the format LB-0001: the prefix LB and a four-digit sequential number.
3. Document Control records the identifier, the logbook's title, the owning function, the equipment, room, or activity, the page count, the issue date, and the recipient in the register.
4. Document Control writes the identifier, the title, and the page count on the logbook cover before issuing it.
5. Use only a logbook issued under this section for a GMP record. Do not keep GMP records in an unregistered notebook.

### 4.2 Make entries

1. Make each entry in indelible ink, at the time the activity occurs, following SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
2. Record the date, the activity performed, the result or observation, and your initials for each entry.
3. Correct an error with a single line through the error that leaves the original entry readable, and initial and date the correction.
4. Mark a page that will not be used with the word VOID written across its face. The page remains in the logbook.
5. Rule a single diagonal line through any blank space remaining after the last entry on a page.
6. Do not remove, tear out, or obscure any page of a logbook.
7. Use one logbook for one equipment item, one room, or one activity. Do not record a second item in the same logbook.

### 4.3 Review logbooks

1. The owning function supervisor reviews each of the function's active logbooks once a month.
2. Sign and date the review beside the last entry reviewed.
3. Have an error found in review corrected under section 4.2 by the person who made the entry, where that person is available.
4. Refer a missing or damaged page to Quality Assurance the same business day it is found.

### 4.4 Close and reconcile a logbook

1. Return a full or superseded logbook to Document Control.
2. Document Control counts the pages in the returned logbook.
3. Document Control compares the count with the page count recorded at issuance and confirms that every page is present or marked VOID.
4. Document Control records the closure and the reconciliation result in the logbook register.
5. Quality Assurance reviews the reconciliation.
6. Document Control archives the closed logbook under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.

### 4.5 Replace a lost or damaged logbook

1. Report a lost or damaged logbook to Quality Assurance the same business day the loss or damage is discovered.
2. Quality Assurance opens a deviation under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations, covering the period for which the logbook's records are unavailable.
3. Document Control issues a replacement logbook with a new identifier and records the lost logbook's identifier against it in the register.
4. Record the lost logbook's identifier and the deviation number in the first entry of the replacement logbook.

## 5. Records

Logbook register entries, issued logbooks, monthly review signatures, and closure reconciliations, retained in FL-EDMS and in the archive under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
