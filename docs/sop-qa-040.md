# SOP-QA-040 — Process and Product Technology Transfer and Comparability

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Assurance

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how the FL-101 process and product are transferred between units, sites, or scales, and how comparability is assessed after a change to the process or product.

## 2. Scope

Applies to process and product technology transfer and to comparability assessment for FL-101 at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Quality Assurance, Manufacturing, Quality Control laboratory, Regulatory Affairs.

Does not cover analytical procedure transfer. Analytical procedure transfer is governed by SOP-QC-007, Analytical Method Transfer and Compendial Verification, not this procedure.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Sending Unit Lead**

- Lead the transfer jointly with the Receiving Unit Lead.
- Provide the process and product information required by the transfer protocol.
- Support execution of the transfer as assigned in the transfer protocol.

**Receiving Unit Lead**

- Lead the transfer jointly with the Sending Unit Lead.
- Prepare the receiving unit to execute the transfer protocol.
- Support preparation of the transfer report.

**Quality Assurance**

- Approve the transfer protocol before transfer work begins.
- Approve the transfer report before the receiving unit manufactures for release.
- Approve the comparability protocol before comparability assessment begins.
- Determine, with Regulatory Affairs, whether process, nonclinical, or clinical bridging is also required.
- Disposition the outcome when a comparability conclusion has failed.

**QA Manager**

- Oversee application of this procedure within Quality Assurance.
- Review the transfer report for approval.
- Review the comparability outcome for disposition.

**Regulatory Affairs**

- Assess filing impact under SOP-QA-004, Change Control, §4.3.
- Determine, with Quality Assurance, whether process, nonclinical, or clinical bridging is also required.
- Record the filing impact assessment in the comparability documentation.

**Study Director**

- Direct analytical comparability studies, per SOP-QC-003, Analytical Procedure Validation, conventions.
- Ensure analytical comparability study work follows the approved comparability protocol.
- Provide the analytical comparability study results for the comparability assessment.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Plan the transfer

1. Identify the transfer as a transfer of the FL-101 process between units, sites, or scales.
2. Assign the Sending Unit Lead for the transfer.
3. Assign the Receiving Unit Lead for the transfer.
4. Confirm that the Sending Unit Lead and the Receiving Unit Lead lead the transfer jointly.
5. Prepare a transfer protocol for the transfer.
6. Include the transfer activities in the transfer protocol.
7. Include the responsibilities for the transfer activities in the transfer protocol.
8. Submit the transfer protocol to Quality Assurance for approval.
9. Obtain Quality Assurance approval of the transfer protocol.
10. Begin transfer work only after Quality Assurance has approved the transfer protocol.
11. Assess risk for the transfer following SOP-QA-009, Quality Risk Management.
12. Record the risk assessment with the transfer documentation.
13. Make any underlying change through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
14. Plan validation at the receiving unit following SOP-QA-016, Process Validation.

### 4.2 Execute the transfer and report

1. Execute the transfer work under the approved transfer protocol.
2. Perform the transfer activities assigned to the sending unit.
3. Perform the transfer activities assigned to the receiving unit.
4. Record the results of the transfer activities.
5. Perform validation at the receiving unit following SOP-QA-016, Process Validation.
6. Record the validation results with the transfer documentation.
7. Prepare a transfer report for the transfer.
8. Include the transfer results in the transfer report.
9. Include the validation results in the transfer report.
10. Submit the transfer report to Quality Assurance for approval.
11. Obtain Quality Assurance approval of the transfer report.
12. Manufacture for release at the receiving unit only after Quality Assurance has approved the transfer report.
13. Retain the approved transfer protocol with the transfer documentation.
14. Retain the approved transfer report with the transfer documentation.

### 4.3 Assess comparability after a change

1. Identify the change to the process or product.
2. Make the underlying change through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
3. Assess risk for the change following SOP-QA-009, Quality Risk Management.
4. Record the risk assessment with the comparability documentation.
5. Prepare a comparability protocol for the changed process or product.
6. Submit the comparability protocol to Quality Assurance for approval.
7. Obtain Quality Assurance approval of the comparability protocol.
8. Assess comparability under the approved comparability protocol.
9. Follow ICH Q5E principles for the comparability assessment.
10. Assess analytical comparability first.
11. Assess analytical comparability against SPEC-101, FL-101 Drug Substance Specification.
12. Assess analytical comparability against the characterization data.
13. Direct the analytical comparability studies through the Study Director.
14. Record the analytical comparability results.
15. Have Quality Assurance and Regulatory Affairs review the analytical comparability results.
16. Have Quality Assurance, with Regulatory Affairs, determine whether process bridging is also required.
17. Have Quality Assurance, with Regulatory Affairs, determine whether nonclinical bridging is also required.
18. Have Quality Assurance, with Regulatory Affairs, determine whether clinical bridging is also required.
19. Record each bridging determination in the comparability documentation.
20. Have Regulatory Affairs assess filing impact under SOP-QA-004, Change Control, §4.3.
21. Record the filing impact assessment in the comparability documentation.

### 4.4 Decide and document the outcome

1. Compile the comparability results in the comparability documentation.
2. State the comparability conclusion in the comparability documentation.
3. Submit the comparability conclusion to Quality Assurance.
4. Record the Quality Assurance review of the comparability conclusion.
5. Use the changed process for released product only when the comparability conclusion has not failed.
6. Stop use of the changed process for released product when the comparability conclusion has failed.
7. Submit a failed comparability outcome to Quality Assurance for disposition.
8. Obtain the Quality Assurance disposition of the failed comparability outcome.
9. Record the Quality Assurance disposition in the comparability documentation.
10. Use the changed process for released product after a failed conclusion only as dispositioned by Quality Assurance.
11. Provide the comparability outcome for the annual product quality review under SOP-QA-021, Annual Product Quality Review.
12. Retain the approved comparability protocol with the comparability documentation.
13. Retain the comparability conclusion with the comparability documentation.

## 5. Records

Records generated under this procedure include approved transfer protocols, transfer reports, risk assessments, validation records for the receiving unit, comparability protocols, analytical comparability study results, bridging determinations, filing impact assessments, comparability conclusions, and Quality Assurance dispositions. These records are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
