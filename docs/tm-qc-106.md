# TM-QC-106 — Bioburden Test, FL-101 Drug Substance

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Control

## 1. Purpose

This method determines the bioburden of FL-101 drug substance bulk by
membrane filtration, following USP <61>, Microbiological Examination of
Nonsterile Products: Microbial Enumeration Tests. The test is performed
on the bulk before sterilizing filtration, as SPEC-QC-101 requires; the
filtration itself is performed under SOP-QA-024, Aseptic Processing and
Media Fills.

## 2. Principle

A defined volume of bulk is passed through a membrane filter that
retains microorganisms. The membrane is placed on growth media and
incubated. Colonies are counted and the count is expressed per 100 mL
of bulk.

## 3. Parameters

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Technique | Membrane filtration, per USP <61> |
| Sample volume | 100 mL of bulk |
| Media | Soybean-casein digest agar; Sabouraud dextrose agar |
| Incubation | Soybean-casein digest agar at 30–35 °C for 5 days; Sabouraud dextrose agar at 20–25 °C for 7 days |
| Negative control | Sterile diluent, filtered and incubated in parallel |
| Media quality | Growth promotion tested under SOP-QC-011 |
| Reporting | Total count as CFU per 100 mL, whole number, per SOP-QA-002 |

## 4. System suitability

1. The negative control shows no growth on either medium.
2. Every medium lot used has passed growth promotion testing under
   SOP-QC-011.

A run that does not meet every criterion in this section yields no
reportable result. Method suitability (recovery of challenge
microorganisms in the presence of the product) is established under
SOP-QC-007, Analytical Method Transfer and Compendial Verification,
before the procedure is used.

## 5. Procedure (summary)

5.1 Draw 100 mL of bulk before sterilizing filtration.
5.2 Filter the sterile diluent for the negative control.
5.3 Filter the 100 mL sample, dividing the filtered sample across two
    membranes, one for each medium.
5.4 Rinse each membrane with sterile diluent.
5.5 Place one membrane on soybean-casein digest agar.
5.6 Place one membrane on Sabouraud dextrose agar.
5.7 Incubate each plate under its conditions from section 3.
5.8 Count the colonies on each plate at the end of its incubation.
5.9 Record all counts on the raw data record.

## 6. Result and reporting

The reported result is the sum of the colony counts on the two media,
reported as CFU per 100 mL, a whole number, per SOP-QA-002. Record per
SOP-QA-001 and SOP-QC-006.

Acceptance of the reported result is per SPEC-QC-101, FL-101 Drug
Substance Specification. This method states no acceptance criterion
for the product.
