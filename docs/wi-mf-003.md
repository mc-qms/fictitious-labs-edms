# WI-MF-003 — Clean/Dirty Hold Time and Equipment Status Tagging

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Manufacturing

## 1. Purpose

This instruction describes how to tag equipment status and how to apply clean and dirty hold times after cleaning and after soiling. It sits under SOP-QA-017, Cleaning Validation.

## 2. Tag cleaned equipment

1. Apply the validated clean hold time established under SOP-QA-017, Cleaning Validation.
2. Use no clean hold time value other than the validated value established under SOP-QA-017, Cleaning Validation.
3. Complete a status tag when cleaning of the equipment is finished.
4. Write CLEAN on the status tag.
5. Write the cleaning date on the status tag.
6. Write the cleaning time on the status tag.
7. Write the clean hold time expiry on the status tag.
8. Attach the completed status tag to the equipment.
9. Check that the tag status agrees with the equipment register in FL-EDMS.
10. Check that the cleaning date and time on the tag agree with the equipment register in FL-EDMS.
11. Check that the clean hold time expiry on the tag agrees with the equipment register in FL-EDMS.
12. Make each tag entry under SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
13. Do not use the equipment if the tag status and the equipment register in FL-EDMS disagree.
14. Refer a disagreement between the tag status and the equipment register in FL-EDMS to Quality Assurance.
15. Wait for Quality Assurance to resolve the difference before using the equipment.
16. Write IN USE on the status tag when the equipment is in use.
17. Attach the IN USE status tag to the equipment in use.
18. Check that an IN USE tag agrees with the equipment register in FL-EDMS.

## 3. Tag soiled equipment

1. Apply the validated dirty hold time established under SOP-QA-017, Cleaning Validation.
2. Use no dirty hold time value other than the validated value established under SOP-QA-017, Cleaning Validation.
3. Complete a status tag when equipment is soiled.
4. Write DIRTY on the status tag.
5. Write the time soiling began on the status tag.
6. Write the time soiling was found on the status tag if the time soiling began is not known.
7. Attach the completed status tag to the soiled equipment.
8. Check that the tag status agrees with the equipment register in FL-EDMS.
9. Check that the time on the tag agrees with the equipment register in FL-EDMS.
10. Make each tag entry under SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
11. Do not use the equipment if the tag status and the equipment register in FL-EDMS disagree.
12. Refer a disagreement between the tag status and the equipment register in FL-EDMS to Quality Assurance.
13. Wait for Quality Assurance to resolve the difference before using the equipment.
14. Write OUT OF SERVICE on the status tag for equipment that is out of service.
15. Attach the OUT OF SERVICE status tag to the equipment.
16. Apply SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance, for defective equipment tagging.

## 4. Respond to an expired hold time

1. Check the clean hold time expiry on the status tag before using cleaned equipment.
2. Do not use equipment that is past its clean hold time.
3. Re-clean equipment that is past its clean hold time before use.
4. Complete a new status tag after the re-cleaning is finished.
5. Write CLEAN on the new status tag.
6. Write the new cleaning date and time on the new status tag.
7. Write the new clean hold time expiry on the new status tag.
8. Check the dirty hold time for equipment held dirty.
9. Report equipment held dirty past its dirty hold time to the Manufacturing Supervisor.
10. Handle a dirty hold time overrun under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
11. Check that the tag status of equipment with a dirty hold time overrun agrees with the equipment register in FL-EDMS.

## 5. Control of this instruction

1. Quality Assurance and the Manufacturing Supervisor approve this instruction and each revision of it.
2. Make each revision through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
