# SOP-RA-002 — Biological Product Deviation Reporting

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Regulatory Affairs

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how Regulatory Affairs reports biological product deviations for FL-101 to CBER. It covers the reporting mechanics from the start of the reporting clock through any follow-up report.

## 2. Scope

Applies to biological product deviation reporting for FL-101 at Fictitious Labs Inc., a US-based biologics organization manufacturing monoclonal antibody drug substance in the United States, in the following functions: Regulatory Affairs, Quality Assurance.

Does not cover the assessment of whether an event is a biological product deviation requiring a report. The assessment of whether an event is a biological product deviation requiring a report is made under SOP-RA-001, Adverse Event Intake and Reporting (and SOP-QA-014, Product Recall, for recall events) and is NOT repeated here; this procedure covers the reporting mechanics only. It also does not disposition product; the underlying deviation remains governed by SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.

Fictitious Labs Inc. is the biologics license applicant and holder for FL-101.

FL-101 is regulated by CBER.

## 3. Responsibilities

**Regulatory Affairs**

- Hosts the safety function for Fictitious Labs Inc.
- Receives events after the reportability assessment under SOP-RA-001, Adverse Event Intake and Reporting.
- Prepares Form FDA 3486.
- Submits Form FDA 3486 to CBER.
- Maintains each report entry in the reporting calendar in FL-EDMS.
- Submits follow-up information under the same case reference.
- Retains the submission confirmation in FL-EDMS.

**Quality Assurance**

- Refers deviation events from handling under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations.
- Refers complaint events from handling under SOP-QA-013, Complaint Handling.
- Refers recall events from assessment under SOP-QA-014, Product Recall.
- Provides the source record for a referred event to Regulatory Affairs.

**QA Manager**

- Makes the final reportability determination under SOP-RA-001, Adverse Event Intake and Reporting.
- Provides the final reportability determination to Regulatory Affairs.

## 4. Procedure

Events reach this procedure from deviation handling under SOP-QA-010, Handling of Deviations, complaint handling under SOP-QA-013, Complaint Handling, and recall assessment under SOP-QA-014, Product Recall, in each case after the reportability assessment under SOP-RA-001, Adverse Event Intake and Reporting.

There is no separate drug safety department at Fictitious Labs Inc.

The safety function sits in Regulatory Affairs.

### 4.1 Start the reporting clock

1. Receive the referred event record from the referring function.
2. Confirm the record identifies the source process.
3. Confirm the QA Manager has made the final reportability determination under SOP-RA-001, Adverse Event Intake and Reporting.
4. Confirm the determination states the event is reportable.
5. Stop the intake check if the determination is not present.
6. Return the record to the referring function if the determination is not present.
7. Record the date Fictitious Labs Inc. acquired information reasonably suggesting that a reportable event occurred.
8. Treat that date as the start of the reporting clock.
9. Calculate the submission deadline as 45 calendar days from that date.
10. Assign a case reference to the report.
11. Enter the case reference in the reporting calendar in FL-EDMS.
12. Enter the submission deadline in the reporting calendar in FL-EDMS.
13. Record the source process in the reporting calendar in FL-EDMS.

### 4.2 Prepare Form FDA 3486

1. Open Form FDA 3486 for the assigned case reference.
2. Enter Fictitious Labs Inc. as the biologics license applicant and holder.
3. Enter FL-101 as the product.
4. Enter the case reference on the form.
5. Describe the event using the source record.
6. Enter the date Fictitious Labs Inc. acquired information reasonably suggesting that a reportable event occurred.
7. Enter the source process for the event.
8. Complete each applicable section of the form.
9. Leave no applicable section blank.
10. Check each entry against the source record.
11. Correct any entry that differs from the source record.
12. Check the completed form for legibility.
13. Save the completed form in FL-EDMS under the case reference.
14. Mark the reporting calendar entry as prepared.

### 4.3 Submit the report

1. Open the reporting calendar entry in FL-EDMS.
2. Confirm the submission deadline shown in the reporting calendar.
3. Confirm the completed Form FDA 3486 matches the case reference.
4. Select a submission route to CBER.
5. Submit Form FDA 3486 through CBER's electronic application or to the CBER Document Control Center.
6. Verify the submission date falls within 45 calendar days of the date Fictitious Labs Inc. acquired information reasonably suggesting that a reportable event occurred, as 21 CFR 600.14 requires.
7. Obtain the submission confirmation.
8. Check the submission confirmation against the case reference.
9. Retain the submission confirmation in FL-EDMS.
10. Record the submission date in the reporting calendar in FL-EDMS.
11. Record the submission route in the reporting calendar in FL-EDMS.
12. Mark the reporting calendar entry as submitted.
13. File the submitted copy of Form FDA 3486 in FL-EDMS under the case reference.

### 4.4 Submit follow-up information

1. Receive information learned after submission.
2. Confirm the information bears on the submitted report.
3. Record the date the follow-up information was received.
4. Retrieve the original report from FL-EDMS.
5. Identify the original case reference.
6. Prepare a follow-up report under the same case reference.
7. Describe the follow-up information in the follow-up report.
8. Check the follow-up report against the source information.
9. Correct any entry that differs from the source information.
10. Submit the follow-up report to CBER through CBER's electronic application or to the CBER Document Control Center.
11. Obtain the follow-up submission confirmation.
12. Retain the follow-up submission confirmation in FL-EDMS.
13. Record the follow-up submission date in the reporting calendar in FL-EDMS.
14. File the follow-up report in FL-EDMS under the same case reference.

## 5. Records

Records created under this procedure include the completed Form FDA 3486 for each report, each follow-up report, each submission confirmation, and the reporting calendar entries in FL-EDMS. The reporting calendar in FL-EDMS is maintained under SOP-RA-001, Adverse Event Intake and Reporting. Each submission confirmation is retained in FL-EDMS under the case reference for the report. Follow-up reports are filed in FL-EDMS under the same case reference as the original report. All records created under this procedure are retained under the retention requirements of SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
