# SOP-QC-014 — Bench Instrument Operation and Daily Checks

**Fictitious Labs Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-02 · Owner: Quality Control

## 1. Purpose

State the routine checks for balances, pH meters, pipettes, and biological safety cabinets, and identify the UV spectrophotometer as instrument UV-02, for use under SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance, and SOP-QC-006, Reference Standards and Laboratory Controls.

## 2. Balances

1. Identify the balance to be used.
2. Check whether the daily check has been performed for the balance.
3. Perform the check daily before first use when the daily check has not been performed.
4. Perform the check with certified weights.
5. Use the balance only after the daily check has been performed.
6. Record the check in the instrument record in FL-EDMS.
7. Record the check in accordance with SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
8. Identify a balance that has failed its check.
9. Take a balance that has failed its check out of service.
10. Take the balance out of service under SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance.
11. Record the failed check in the instrument record in FL-EDMS.
12. Record the out-of-service status in the instrument record in FL-EDMS.
13. Record the balance checked in the instrument record in FL-EDMS.

## 3. pH meters

1. Identify the pH meter to be used.
2. Check whether the daily calibration has been performed for the pH meter.
3. Calibrate the pH meter daily before first use when the daily calibration has not been performed.
4. Perform a three-point calibration.
5. Use the pH meter only after the daily calibration has been performed.
6. Record the calibration in the instrument record in FL-EDMS.
7. Record the calibration in accordance with SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
8. Store the probe in storage solution.
9. Do not store the probe dry.
10. Check that the probe is in storage solution after use.
11. Return the probe to storage solution after use.
12. Record the check in the instrument record in FL-EDMS.

## 4. Pipettes

1. Identify the pipette to be checked.
2. Identify the pipette in FL-EDMS.
3. Read the performance check interval recorded for the pipette in FL-EDMS.
4. Check whether a performance check is due at the recorded interval.
5. Perform the pipette performance check at the interval recorded for the pipette in FL-EDMS.
6. Perform the performance check under SOP-QA-020, Calibration and Preventive Maintenance.
7. Record the performance check in the instrument record in FL-EDMS.
8. Record the performance check in accordance with SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
9. Record the pipette checked in the instrument record in FL-EDMS.
10. Record the interval applied in the instrument record in FL-EDMS.

## 5. Biological safety cabinets

1. Identify the biological safety cabinet to be used.
2. Start the biological safety cabinet.
3. Allow the cabinet to stabilize after start-up.
4. Use the cabinet only after it has stabilized.
5. Select a disinfecting agent from the cleaning program in SOP-QA-034, Routine Facility Cleaning, Sanitization, and Sporicidal Rotation.
6. Disinfect the cabinet before use with the selected agent.
7. Complete the disinfection before use.
8. Disinfect the cabinet after use with the selected agent.
9. Complete the disinfection after use.
10. Record the disinfection in the instrument record in FL-EDMS.
11. Record the disinfection in accordance with SOP-QA-001, Good Documentation Practices.
12. Record the agent used in the instrument record in FL-EDMS.

## 6. Control of this procedure

1. Quality Assurance and the Quality Control Supervisor approve this procedure and each revision of it.
2. Make each revision through SOP-QA-004, Change Control.
